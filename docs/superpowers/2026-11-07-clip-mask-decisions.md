# Shape clips as masked groups — decisions (prefix `CM-`)

Rulings for R4-5 PR C: an ellipse, a path, an even-odd or a non-antialiased `clipShape`, and two crossing rounded clips, paint into an offscreen group that is multiplied by the shape's coverage before it is drawn back (**divergences 91 and 92 retired; 224 added**; live 209, next label 226 because 225 is a live row). Branch `feat/clip-mask` (master `3c7eadb9`). Record 220. Evidence: the probe `docs/probes/swiftui-clip-mask.swift` (arms `CM0`..`CM7`; compiled, 2026-11-06). Next unused id: `CM-I`. Carried: a mask scene may not hold a group, backdrop or surface (a precondition); a crossing at a flattening entry is one masked group per leaf, not per scope (`CM-B`); the continuous-corner shape of divergence 90 could now be drawn exactly through a path mask and is not.

## CM-A: the mask as a group multiplier

An ellipse, a path (nonzero or even-odd) or a non-antialiased clip paints its content into an offscreen group (`Frame.paintWithMask`: a `PaintScope` of kind `.composite` with a `mask` field, so no `PaintScope.Kind` was added) and the group's target is multiplied by the shape's coverage. `Scene.insert(_:group:layer:transform:filter:mask:)` carries the mask as a second finalized scene (`Scene.groupMasks`, parallel to `groupFilters`; rects and images only, a precondition; permuted by `finalize()`, cleared by `clear()`). The mask scene is drawn on the GPU where it can be: an ellipse or the second rounded rect of a crossing is one `MUIRect` whose SDF is exact at any transform; only a `Path` clip is a CPU raster, through the existing `pathImage` / `RasterCache` route. A rounded rectangle, circle or capsule is today's per-primitive mask: no group. **How `TE-AD` is met**: no record changes its stride and no shader source is new. The mask pass is `group_tint_fragment` (`tint.fragment`, `TINT_STAGE`) at tint (1, 1, 1, 1) over a **destination-in blend** (source factor zero, destination factor source alpha, colour and alpha): `dst *= mask.a`. The new state is one blend state on each backend (`BackdropPipelines.mask` on Metal, `g->mask` on SDL); `replay.hlsl` and the shadercross blobs are unchanged and parity frame 18 judges it.

## CM-B: crossing rounded clips (divergence 92 retired)

`Frame.clipsCross` is true where `Frame.intersect` takes its last square-box fallback with a radius non-zero **and the square box reaches a corner cut** of either rounded rect; a pair whose box misses every cut is exact as pushed and takes no group. At a `pushClip` (`paintClipped`) the crossing opens a mask scope whose mask is one rect (the new clip as its outline, the clip in force as its `contentMask`, so coverage is the product of both rounded rects). At a flattening effect's entry (`cutToEntryClip`) the primitive keeps its own mapped mask uncut and joins a masked group whose mask is the entry clip. The pushed clip stays the square box, so hit testing and every primitive's own mask are unchanged. Probes `CM2`, `CM2b`, `CM2c` read white at the square-box corners: the exact intersection is SwiftUI's.

## CM-C: opacity

Probe `CM6` reads per leaf (191, 64, 128): an opacity outside a clip fades each leaf. The mask mark carries alpha 1, `CapturedPrimitive.multiplyAlpha` multiplies the colours when the outermost mark is a mask, and the quad is drawn at opacity 1.

## CM-D: what a group cannot hold

A scope holding a backdrop (a material or glass) gets no mark and keeps the bounding clip (divergence 224, `masksFlattenedBackdrop`); a masked group over 8192 on a side is drawn per primitive under the bounding clip (`masksFlattenedOversize`); a surface is allowed. A lone untransformed plain rect that holds the whole ellipse is that ellipse rect (`masksBakedToEllipse`). A `Deferred` inside a mask scope is not masked: `withoutRenderEffects` pushes its barrier for a mask scope as for a composite one.

## CM-E: hit testing and accessibility

Both are the bounding rect (`TE-AQ` item 4), unchanged: prepaint is untouched and registers hitboxes under the bounding clip. Pinned by `anEllipseClipStillClipsHitboxesToItsBoundingRect`.

## CM-F: mapping

Every site that edits a primitive's geometry maps the mask a mark carries (`CapturedPrimitive.mapMarkMasks`): `RenderEffect.apply`, `cutToEntryClip`, `insertFilteredGroup`, `RetainedTranslation.movedLeaf`, the flag-setting loop of `processScopes` (`innerMask`, `outerMaskInner`, `GroupMark.maskDepth`), `replayed(mask:layer:)` (drag previews) and `Scene.capturedGroup(at:)` (ghosts). `bakeGroupAlpha` and `insertIntoScene`'s belt drop a mask with its mark; `liftSharedMarks` stops at a masked mark so `.clipShape(E).shadow/.blur` take the filtered-group route with the masked group inside. A mask scope inside an `.equatable()` occurrence is an inner scope: a moved occurrence misses with `innerScope`; a replay at the same position re-groups through `insertLeaves`.

## CM-G: SDL, D3D12 and the fixture

`ReplayGroup` gains `mask_run_start` and `mask_run_count` (the mask's runs follow the group's own in the frame's run array). `render_groups` in `SDLBridge.c`, after the content pass and **before** the filter, acquires a scratch slot of the group's size, draws the mask runs into it under the group's projection, then runs one LOAD pass over the target with `g->mask` (the `tint` stage, destination-in, tint 1). `make_pipeline` takes a blend mode (`BLEND_NONE`, `BLEND_OVER`, `BLEND_DST_IN`); `ensure_backdrop_pipelines` builds `g->mask` with the other portable pass pipelines, so without them (or without a scratch, `maskPoolFailsAllocation`) the group draws **unmasked by name**: `mask_failures`. Counters `lastMaskPasses`, `lastMaskFailures`. `SDLGroupSet` and `FlatFrame` append a mask scene's records and runs after its group's with transform indices shifted like any record's (the two flattenings must stay equal, `GO-F`); `SDLGroupSet.sharedTextures` includes a mask's textures. **Fixture version 9**: per group after the version-8 body, `u32 hasMask` and, when 1, a second nested body (versions 2-8 load unmasked); a mask body holding a group run is refused. Parity frame 18 (`--expect 19`): an ellipse clip, a triangle and an even-odd path clip, crossing rounded clips (`pushClip` and offset-entry forms), an ellipse clip under a rotation and under `rotation3DEffect`, a masked group inside an opacity group and under a blur. Measured on SDL-over-MSL: 0 differing pixels, max channel delta 0; Linux Vulkan and Windows D3D12 are judged in CI only.

## CM-H: divergences
