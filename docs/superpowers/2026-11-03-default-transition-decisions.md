1. **The rule:** a paint replay stands for the paint its record was made from, so it writes what that paint wrote into `MatchedGeometryStore`: each group's `displayed` (set to its `target`, where a live paint draws a group not in flight) and, where the replay emitted the record's count, its `sequences` range at the replay's base. Same shape as `XF-I`'s unit ranges and `RetainedRecord.ranges`.
2. **The mechanism:** `MatchedGeometryStore.noted` (every `noteSequences` and every `renote`, cleared in `endFrame`) is read by the recorder from `SubtreeRecorder.matchedFrom` into `RetainedRecord.matched` (ranges relative to the window's `sequenceFrom`); `moved(to:)` copies it, `differences(from:)` compares it (`"matched groups"`), and `replayPaint` calls `renote(_:base:)` with `base: nil` when the emitted count differs (`displayed` still re-noted, nothing appended). `renote` appends to `noted` when given a base, so an outer record inherits an inner replay. No journal event is owed (the store is never a `StateTable` slot; an occurrence with a matched group is graft-refused) and `everyStateWriteSiteIsClassified` is unchanged. `rollbackBuild` needs no clear of `noted`: an abandoned attempt's entries sit below the redo recorder's `matchedFrom` (the smallest spelling; the clear was green when removed, MR6).
3. **Why `displayed = target` is sound:** a live flight never replays. Its `noteActiveAnimation` taints the recorder (`anInFlightInsertionIsNotReplayedFrozen`'s reasoning), so a record that replays is a group drawn at its target. Pinned by the interrupted arm and the second swap after landing, both equal to the retention-off oracle.

## XF-A: what SwiftUI does to an unannotated insertion and removal (row 98's evidence; measured)

1. **The fade** (`XF0`, `XF3`): a default opacity `+1` on insertion and `-1` on removal, one animator per view, in a ZStack, VStack, HStack and a root `Group`; no transaction, nothing; first render, nothing (`XF9a`); `.transition(.identity)` on the content suppresses it (`X15b`'). A nested `.transition` is inert and the unit still fades (`X15`'). An `if`/`else` swap is two animators, `-1` and `+1` (`XF0`, `W10`').
2. **The unit is each member of the `if`, not the `if`** (`XF4`): `if f { A; B }` gives **two** opacity animators. **A mixed unit is not all-or-nothing** (`XF5`, `XF5b`): with `A.transition(.move(edge: .leading))` the annotated member animates its own move and the unannotated one **still fades** (one opacity animator); with `.identity` on A only B fades. The plan's design step 4 ("a mixed unit gets no default") is contradicted.
3. **A `ForEach` element fades** (`XF6`, `XF6b`): one opacity animator per inserted or removed element, plus the shifting siblings' geometry in a stack.
4. **A changed `.id` is a cross-fade** (`XF7`): both a `-1` and a `+1` opacity animator; under a 0.6 s ramp `appear` is early and `disappear` is late. It is the one default transition not written as an `if`.
5. **An `AnyView` swap does NOT fade** (`XF8a`, `XF8b`, with or without `.transition(.opacity)` inside): nothing reaches the recorder, and `onDisappear` runs in the swap step before `onAppear` (`XF8e`, `XF8f`, `AV6a`, `AV6b`). `if f { AnyView(A) }` fades like any `if` (`XF8c`). **Stop condition 3 did not fire**: divergence 212 and `$erased` stand as ruled.
6. **The lifecycle** (`T1u`, `A4u`, `T4u`, `T1e`, `XF7`): an unannotated removal under an animation delays `onDisappear` to the end of the animation exactly as `T1`'s `.transition(.opacity)` does; an `if`/`else` swap keeps `appear` first and delays the old branch's `onDisappear`; re-insertion mid-removal fires neither callback; an `if` whose content draws nothing (`Color.clear`) delays it too, with or without a transition. **Stop condition 4 did not fire**; `LC-F`/`LC-H`'s parking is what SwiftUI does for a default removal as well.
7. **Not animated** (`XF9`): a `LazyVStack` and a `List` row entering the window under an animated `scrollTo` (origin 0 to 2400 and 0 to 3610): nothing reaches the recorder. `List` data insertion: nothing reached the recorder, which is not evidence about `NSTableView`'s own row animation (unmeasured). **Stop condition 8 did not fire for the scroll.**
8. **Reduce Motion** (`XF11`): the default insert `+1` and remove `-1` are unchanged.

## XF-B: where the leaver is drawn (measured; contradicts a recollection of the plan)

1. **The bitmap and the layer tree both work** (`XF1`, `XF2`, controls valid): `cacheDisplay` over a white `.background` reads a linear ramp for an animated `.opacity(0 -> 1)`, a default and a `.opacity` insertion, and the mirror ramp for a default and a `.opacity` removal; `.identity` reads full red at once. `host.layer` is a tree of `CALayer`s; a static `.opacity(0.5)` reads 0.50, an animated one reads 0.33 at 0.4 s of a 1.2 s ramp, and a removal ghost is a layer reading 0.67. **Stop condition 14 did not fire** (both instruments passed).
2. **The ghost is the FIRST sublayer of its container's run** (`XF10`, `XF2zb`, `XF2zc`; leaves given `.opacity(0.99)` so each is its own layer): `ZStack { blue; if red }` removal reads `[background, ghost, blue]`, with a third always-present leaf above red `[background, ghost, blue, green]`, in a `VStack` with red first `[ghost, blue]`, and nested (`blue; ZStack { if red; yellow }; green`) `[background, blue, ghost, yellow, green]`. The pixel arms agree: the overlap with a blue sibling reads pure blue from the first sample whether the red was above or below it at rest. Annotated (`.transition(.opacity)`) and unannotated read the same. Insertion stays in place (`XF10d`).
3. **Consequences.** Neither MetalUI's present rule (every ghost above the rest of its layer, `Transition.swift:93`) nor the plan's "in place" (design step 8) matches: SwiftUI drops a leaver to the **bottom of its own container's run**. **Stop condition 9 did not fire as written** (it names "on top"), but it fires in spirit: the plan's step 8 and its new row are derived from a recollection that is refuted for a leaver that was above a sibling. PR B decides the ghost's position from this item.
4. **A sibling backdrop hides a removal ghost.** An earlier run drew the white backdrop as a sibling leaf and read white at every sample of every removal: the ghost sits below it. Every pixel arm here uses `.background`.
5. **Unmeasured:** the look in a real window; whether a compositor reorders layers the bitmap does not (no Screen Recording, locked); a leaver nested more than one container deep beyond `XF2zc`.

## XF-C: `Animatable` (evidence for `VA-`, rulings owed to PR D)

1. **`_TrimmedShape.AnimatableData`** is `AnimatablePair<S.AnimatableData, AnimatablePair<CGFloat, CGFloat>>` (the SDK interface and `VA3a`: `P<P<Empty, P<F, F>>, ...>` for `Circle().trim`). **Its fractions are scaled by 128 in the data** (`VA3a2`, `VA3d`, `VA3e`: an end fraction 0 to 1 reads delta 128, a start 0 to 0.5 reads 64). A MetalUI pair that interpolates fractions linearly is indistinguishable on screen; a spring's settle threshold or a velocity projection that depends on the magnitude is not.
2. **A custom `Shape` animates through `animatableData`** (`VA1`), snaps without it (`VA2`); a wrapper around a trim that declares nothing snaps (`VA3b`), one that forwards a `Double` animates (`VA3c`), one that forwards the trim's own data animates the trim and the base together (`VA3d`), and a trim of a trim nests (`VA3e`).
3. **A retarget on one component re-bases the WHOLE pair** (`VA4`): a new `P<Double, Double>` animator (not a `Double`) whose delta covers both components, `b` included. **Stop condition 10 (per-component) did not fire**; `PaintAnimatedShape` may go in PR D.
4. **A `View & Animatable` runs its body at interpolated values and its children are proposed interpolated widths** (`VA5`, against the control that is proposed only the final width). **`VA5`'s stop did not fire; PR E stands.** An `Animatable & Equatable` view with `.equatable()` runs its body in flight like the plain one; settled it runs none (`VA7`). **The `VA7` stop did not fire.**
5. **Retarget continuity** (`VA6`): position continuous, the new ramp from the current value. **The velocity projection of the plan's PR D design is not seen** (`VA6c`): `a`'s slope across a retarget of the other component read 3.58/s before and 3.17/s after (a 30 ms window mean, timing); the design's "perpendicular component dropped" is unsupported and should not be built without a stronger arm.
6. **Keyframes over a custom `Animatable` struct** compile and interpolate (`VA8`); a `Float` track does **not** compile in SwiftUI (`Float` is a `VectorArithmetic` but not `Animatable`, `VT11`, `VT13`, `VT16`), so row 169's "`Float` tracks compile the same" is a MetalUI extra, not parity.
7. **Isolation** (the SDK `.swiftinterface` and `VT2`..`VT7`): `Animatable` and `Shape` carry no isolation, `View`, `ViewModifier` and `Transition` are `@MainActor`. SwiftUI's own spelling `struct V: View, Animatable { var animatableData … }` **compiles in Swift 5 mode and is an error in Swift 6 mode** ("conformance of 'V' to protocol 'Animatable' crosses into main actor-isolated code"); `nonisolated var animatableData` compiles in both. A MetalUI `Element: @MainActor` with a nonisolated `Animatable` requirement would behave identically, so the isolation difference need not be a divergence if the requirement is nonisolated. PR D opens with the spike that settles it; this is only the SwiftUI side.

## XF-F: where a removal ghost is drawn (R4-3 PR B1, record 205)

1. **A ghost is the first thing in its container's run.** The container is the nearest ancestor of the ghost's position (`key.parent`, the id the removed content sat at) that passed through `Frame.recordElementBounds`: every `Element` group member, every modifier layer, `AnyElement`'s group entry and the root. A `Component` and a conditional's slot never record, so they are transparent. Measured in SwiftUI: `XF10`, `XF2zb`, `XF2zc` (record 204); annotated and unannotated alike (`XF2z`, `XF10c`); an insertion stays in place (`XF10d`, unchanged). **No divergence row**: the rule is the same code path for the annotated ghost MetalUI draws today, so a row would describe something this PR fixes.
2. **Resolved once, by a frame-local set.** `afterLayout` sets `Frame.pendingGhostAnchors` only when it makes a ghost; while it is set `recordElementBounds` also inserts each id into a set; after prepaint and before paint `TransitionStore.resolveAnchors(seen:)` walks each unanchored ghost's `.parent` chain to the first id in the set and stores it on the `Ghost`. A steady frame pays one flag read per element prepaint. Sound under prepaint replay because an ancestor of a flipped conditional ran its build, and a descendant that ran kills its ancestors' records (`SB-AC`); the ledger records no element bounds without `recordsElementBounds`, so a replay could not feed the set anyway.
3. **Painted by `Frame.paintAnchoredGhosts(at:)`**, behind `Frame.hasGhostAnchors` (one flag read per element paint): `Element.paintGroup` before `paint(...)` when the element is not a `StyledElement`; `paintDecorationUncaptured`'s content closure, so a styled container's own fill stays below the ghost and its children above; `ModifiedContent.paintLayer`'s innermost `inside()`; `AnyElement`'s group entry; the root in `Frame.render`. A ghost is inserted with `insertIntoScene`, so it bypasses every live scope, as a ghost always has (frozen at its last place; stated in `Transition.swift`).
4. **A ghost drawn into a run taints the paint recorders** (`retainLog?.taint("ghostInRun")`): a direct insert bypasses `RetainLog`, so an enclosing `.equatable()` occurrence would record without it and replay the flip frame's paint, frozen, over a ghost that has since moved. While a ghost lives that occurrence builds, prepaints and paints fresh each frame (counted by name), its siblings still graft, and **no frame is redone** (measured, record 205 section 3). Ghosts drawn after the root never had the problem.
5. **A ghost no hook reached is drawn after the root, as before, and counted** in `TransitionStore.lastFrameUnanchoredGhosts` (a hidden container; a container that left): 0 in every ordinary tree, a pinned work counter. A finished ghost is neither drawn nor counted; `paintGhosts` still prunes.
6. **A redo restores the draw state**: `Ghost.anchor` and `Ghost.drawn` live in the ghost, so `Checkpoint`/`rollbackBuild` put them back with the ghosts array (`RK-M`).
7. **Unmeasured, stated so:** the look in a real window; a leaver nested more than one container beyond `XF2zc`; a modifier chain between the container and the conditional (MetalUI's rule: the nearest prepainted ancestor); an erased container whose wrapped element is a `StyledElement` draws the ghost below that element's own fill (the hook is at the position's id, not the wrapped content's).

## XF-G: a ghost always exists (R4-3 PR B1, record 205)

1. **An annotated removal of content that drew nothing parks its disappearance.** `T1e2`: an annotated `Color.clear` delays `onDisappear`; `T1e`: so does an empty unannotated `if`. MetalUI skipped the ghost when the capture was empty (`!previous.captures.isEmpty`) or the group had no rect, so the callback ran at once. Both guards are dropped; a missing `surfaceRect` is a zero rect no primitive reads. The ghost draws nothing but wakes the display link and holds the lifecycle for its animation.
2. **The lifecycle is then right across an atlas rebuild**: `forgetCapturedPrimitives` empties captures and a removal after it is a ghost that draws nothing, not no ghost (`GL-D` item 3's comment amended).
3. **Only annotated ghosts exist in this PR.** The unannotated default fade (and its ghost, key and slice) is PR B2; nothing here makes an unannotated `if` fade or park.

## XF-H: the default fade (R4-3 PR B2, record 206; row 98 narrowed)

1. **Which conditionals are units.** A unit fades by default when an animation inserts or removes it: a **builder** `if` (`OptionalGroup(builder:)`, set by `ElementBuilder.buildOptional` and so by `ProposalContentBuilder`), **either branch of an `if`/`else` or `switch`** (`EitherGroup` is only ever made by the builders' `buildEither`, so every branch is a unit and the enum needed no flag; a hand-written `.first(_)` fades too) and **a non-lazy `ForEach` element** (`noteLoopElement`). A framework slot built with `OptionalGroup(_:)` (a popover, a control's label, the drag preview), a `for` loop's iteration (`ArrayGroup`; MetalUI's own loop, SwiftUI's `ViewBuilder` has none) and a lazy `ForEach` (`LZ-H`: it notes no loop) are not. The grep of builder `if`s in `Sources/MetalUI/` (a temporary deprecation on `buildOptional`/`buildEither`, every file recompiled) found two sites, both one statement: `ColorPickerPanel.content`'s `if supportsOpacity` (a constant of the panel, never flipped). No control body needed the non-builder spelling.
2. **The rule** (`XF0`, `XF3`, `XF4`, `XF6`, `XF2`): default opacity +-1 per unit, linear in activeness (0.33 inserted and 0.67 removed at 0.4 of a 1.2 s linear animation), on the transaction at the conditional; `.identity` suppresses it (`X15b`'), a nested `.transition` is inert and the unit still fades (`X15`'), no transaction and the first render do nothing (`XF9a`), Reduce Motion unchanged (`XF11`). A **mixed unit**: each annotated member keeps its own transition, carved out of the unit's range (removal) or exempt from the unit's fade scope (insertion), and the others fade (`XF5`, `XF5b`); a unit whose members are all annotated makes no default ghost. **No `Transition` or shader change** (`TE-AD` is not triggered): a ghost's atoms are `[.opacity]` and an insertion's scope is a `RenderEffect` with the same atoms.
3. **At rest nothing is captured.** A unit's paint reads the scene's emission sequence before and after its content and appends `(unit, start, end)` to a flat array (`Frame.beginConditionalUnit`/`endConditionalUnit`; `Scene.nextEmissionSequence`, `package`); under a `.composite` scope the range is empty (the leaves re-emit later, not contiguously). `TransitionStore.endFrame(scene:)` swaps the array and keeps the frame's **unfinalized** scene, only while a unit painted (`finalizedScene()` already copies, so no extra copy; a tree without a unit retains nothing). A removal slices that scene (`Scene.primitives(inSequences:)`, a binary search per kind merged by sequence; `capturedPrimitive(_:at:)` rebuilds each record with its layer and its transform carried by value, `innerMask: false`) minus the annotated members' ranges (`Record.sequences`, set by `TransitionGroup` around its content).
4. **`afterLayout`** (`advanceDefaultUnits`), gated on `notedAnimationThisFrame` (set by every note with an animation and by `replayEvaluated`) or a standing ghost: an **insertion** is a produced unit whose evaluator was evaluated last frame, not produced last frame, with an animation now (activeness from the ghost's when one is standing); a **removal** iterates `previousProduced`, not the ranges: a unit whose evaluator was evaluated now and which is not produced now, with an animation, **always** makes a ghost, whose slice may be empty (`T1e`: parking is independent of drawing and of retention). A removal during an insertion starts its ghost at activeness 0 because the slice has the insertion's alpha baked in.
5. **The ghost** has the key `.child(of: unit, at: 0, name: "$default-transition")` in the shared `ghosts` array, so `XF-F`'s anchor (the key's grandparent chain), the redo `Checkpoint`, `liveCount` and `forgetCapturedPrimitives` apply unchanged. An insertion's `.fade` scope (`PaintScope.Kind.fade`, **non-capturing**, opened by `beginConditionalUnit` only while `insertions` holds the unit, with `noteActiveAnimation()`, never `requestAnotherFrame()`) is added to the taint check of `processScopes` (an occurrence holding one cannot record) and is `suspended` (a flag; the scope stays on the stack, so no recorder height or index moves) around an annotated member of its unit.
6. **Unmeasured, stated:** how it looks in a real window (human group `XF`); a ghost is frozen at its last place and bypasses every live scope (a scroll or theme change during the fade does not move it); a ghost primitive that a `Deferred`, a glass container or a `.compositingGroup()` emitted later than the unit's range (nothing is faded there; timing still parks); a leaver nested more than one container deep beyond `XF2zc`.

## XF-I: retained replay of the ranges (R4-3 PR B2, record 206)

1. **The previous frame is usually a replay**, so the ranges are carried by the retained records: a recorder remembers `TransitionStore.ranges.count` and the scene sequence where its paint window opened; the record keeps the ranges its window noted **relative to that sequence** (`RetainedRecord.ranges`) and the count it emitted (`emitted`); `Frame.replayPaint` re-notes them at the sequence it starts at and **drops them when it emitted another count** (the ghost then draws nothing and still parks). `differences(from:)` compares both (verify mode), `moved(to:)` copies them, `rollbackBuild` and `forgetCapturedPrimitives` clear the frame's array, and the atlas rebuild drops the kept scene (a captured glyph names an atlas slot, `GL-D` item 3).
2. **The graft is unmoved**: `buildActivity` reads `records.count + capturedThisFrame`, which a default unit changes by nothing, so `transitionGroup` never refuses a graft for a default unit; the journal event `.transitionProduced` gained the `fades` flag (the same writes, no new event) and the graft replays it.

## XF-J: lifecycle of a default ghost (R4-3 PR B2, record 206)

1. **Parking is by the unit**: a default ghost's position is its unit, so `liveGhosts` parks every disappearance below it (`LC-H`), as an annotated one's position does; measured in SwiftUI `T1u`, `A4u` (the swap keeps `appear` first and delays the old branch), `T4u` (re-insertion fires neither callback), `T1e` (an empty `if` delays too).
2. **A `.identity` member of a mixed unit is exempt** (`TransitionStore.Ghost.exempt`, read where `Lifecycle.endFrame` picks the ghost): its disappearance parks under no ghost and runs in the swap step. The timing of `XF5b` itself is unmeasured: predicted by analogy to `X7`, pinned by `anIdentityMembersDisappearIsNotParked`.

## XF-K: a matched-geometry handoff until PR C (R4-3 PR B2, record 206; **replaced by `XF-M`**)

1. **A flight replaces the cross-fade**: when a handoff starts a flight, the leaver's member is carved out of the unit's default ghost (it draws nothing, the ghost still parks) and the newcomer's default insertion is dropped, so the card flies opaque from the leaver's frame as it did, instead of fading in from 0 beside a leaver fading in place. A handoff with no flight (start equals target), a non-source leaver, another id or another namespace is an ordinary cross-fade. PR C replaces this with the leaver moving as it fades (row 897).
2. A matched group records the scene range its content drew in every claimed paint (`MatchedGeometryStore.noteSequences`), flight or not.

## XF-M: the leaver of a matched swap moves as it fades (R4-3 PR C, record 207; row 897 narrowed)

1. **The rule** (probes `S1`, `S12`, `S9`, `S10`, `S13`, `S2`, readings in the recording of `swiftui-matched-geometry.swift`): both views cross-fade and the leaver travels to the group's current source with **its own size** (S1; S12 for a non-source leaver, S9 for `.position`); `.size` alone does not move it (S10), no source does not (S13), `.identity` leaves no ghost (S2). The distance is the delta between the anchor points of the source's laid-out rectangle and of where the leaver was drawn last frame (`displayed`), in points.
2. **The mechanism**: `MatchedGeometryStore.afterLayout` calls `TransitionStore.leaverMoves(transitionKey:sequences:dx:dy:frame:)` for every record claimed last frame and not now, after the handoff loop and independent of any handoff or flight. An **annotated** leaver's standing ghost gains `.translate(sizeX: 0, sizeY: 0, x: dx, y: dy)` when its atoms contain `.opacity`. An **unannotated** leaver's primitives (the scene sequence range its group drew, `Record.sequences`) are carved out of the default ghost(s) holding them into a ghost of their own (key `.child(of: leaverPosition, at: 0, name: "$matched-leaver")`, atoms `[.opacity, .translate]`, the donor's animation, start and activeness); the donor's `exempt` gains the leaver's position so the leaver's `onDisappear` parks on the leaver's ghost. The atom shares the ghost's activeness: the fade and the move are **one curve**. `RenderEffect` already applies a translate (`Transition.swift:264`): no renderer, shader or `replay.hlsl` change.

## XF-N: a replayed occurrence re-notes its matched groups (R4-3c, record 211; closes `XF-M` item 6)

1. **The rule:** a paint replay stands for the paint its record was made from, so it writes what that paint wrote into `MatchedGeometryStore`: each group's `displayed` (set to its `target`, where a live paint draws a group not in flight) and, where the replay emitted the record's count, its `sequences` range at the replay's base. Same shape as `XF-I`'s unit ranges and `RetainedRecord.ranges`.
2. **The mechanism:** `MatchedGeometryStore.noted` (every `noteSequences` and every `renote`, cleared in `endFrame`) is read by the recorder from `SubtreeRecorder.matchedFrom` into `RetainedRecord.matched` (ranges relative to the window's `sequenceFrom`); `moved(to:)` copies it, `differences(from:)` compares it (`"matched groups"`), and `replayPaint` calls `renote(_:base:)` with `base: nil` when the emitted count differs (`displayed` still re-noted, nothing appended). `renote` appends to `noted` when given a base, so an outer record inherits an inner replay. No journal event is owed (the store is never a `StateTable` slot; an occurrence with a matched group is graft-refused) and `everyStateWriteSiteIsClassified` is unchanged. `rollbackBuild` needs no clear of `noted`: an abandoned attempt's entries sit below the redo recorder's `matchedFrom` (the smallest spelling; the clear was green when removed, MR6).
3. **Why `displayed = target` is sound:** a live flight never replays. Its `noteActiveAnimation` taints the recorder (`anInFlightInsertionIsNotReplayedFrozen`'s reasoning), so a record that replays is a group drawn at its target. Pinned by the interrupted arm and the second swap after landing, both equal to the retention-off oracle.
