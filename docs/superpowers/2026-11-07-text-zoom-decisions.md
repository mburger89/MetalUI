# Text under transforms — decisions (prefix `TZ-`)

Rulings for R4-5 PR E: text under a uniform `scaleEffect` is rasterized at the scale it is drawn at (**divergence 106 narrowed**), and a clip between two non-flattening effects is a masked group (**divergence 109 retired**; **224** and **1065** amended). Live 208 (209 - 1), next label 226 (no label is added). Branch `feat/text-zoom` (master `9116adff`). Record 222. Evidence: the probe `docs/probes/swiftui-text-under-transform.swift` (arms `Z0`..`Z7`, `NC0`..`NC5`; compiled `ImageRenderer`, 2026-11-07, two byte-identical runs). Next unused id: `TZ-G`. Supersedes `GX-L` (text "is resampled, not re-rasterized") and `GX-G`'s clip bullet (a clip between nested effects is its bounding box).

## TZ-A: a glyph is re-keyed where it enters the scene

**What SwiftUI does.** Under `scaleEffect` text is re-rasterized at the effective scale, per axis, with no cap seen (`Z0` reproduces T15b: 326 partial pixels for 20 pt at 2x, 298 for real 40 pt, 1237 for a bilinear upscale; `Z1` 1.5x: 246 against 237 and 685; `Z6` `x: 2, y: 1`: 227 against 215 vector and 512 resample; `Z7` 8x and 20x: 578 against 477 native, 1443 against 1443). Under `rotationEffect` it is a **vector** (`Z3` 187 partials against 194 for the CoreGraphics vector and 303 for a bilinear rotation of the upright raster; `Z4` either order of scale and rotation is 0 px apart). A projection alone is a resample of the 1x raster; a projection of a scaled text rasterizes at the scale (`Z5`, `Z5b`).

**What MetalUI does.** A glyph is emitted exactly as before, with its device-scale `GlyphKey`, slot and bounds. `Frame.insertIntoScene`'s `.glyph` arm (and so every route into a scene: the root, `collectingIntoFreshScene` for opacity, filtered and masked groups, a gradient text's ink mask, ghosts, drag previews, retained replays through live scopes, `RZ-A`) calls `Frame.rekeyedGlyph(_:)` (`TextZoom.swift`):

1. `f = bounds.width / slot.width`: the flattening scale already applied to the glyph's bounds. **The raster scale comes from `f` alone. The transform contributes 1** (`Z5`: a projection alone is a resample; the plan's σ over the projective Jacobian was refuted by the probe and dropped). A scale outside a rotation does not flatten (the rotation between keeps it a transform), so it is `f = 1`: the glyph keeps the device raster (`Z4` says SwiftUI is vector-exact there, so it is 106's residue).
2. `k = TextZoom.rasterScale(f, emPixels:)`: the ladder `2^(n/8)` (nine percent a rung, so a resample of at most 4.4 percent; rungs within 1/16 of an octave of 1 are 1, so a 3 percent scale is left alone), floored at 1/4 and, when it enlarges, capped at 256 device pixels of em (`TZ-C`). `Z1` shows SwiftUI exact at 1.5x; the ladder is MetalUI's compromise for an animated zoom and stays in 106's residue.
3. `k == 1` (the common case, text at its device scale): the glyph is inserted as emitted, and **no dictionary is read** (`glyphRekeyLookups` is 0; `RT-M`'s images hold).
4. Otherwise the glyph's key is read from the atlas's slot-to-key map, its pen from the bounds (`penX = bounds.x + f * (-left + variant / 4)`, `baseline = bounds.y + f * top`), and the new key is `GlyphKey(font, glyph, size, subpixelVariant, scaleFactor: scaleFactor * k)` with the variant from `GlyphImage.subpixelPlacement(forDeviceX: penX * r)`, `r = k / f` (the one rule, `PT-C`). The new bounds are the new slot's rectangle divided by `r`, so with no transform and `k == f` the glyph is drawn 1:1 and pixel-aligned, as real text of that size is.

**No `CapturedPrimitive`, `TextSystem`, bridge, shader or fixture change.** `GlyphKey` already had `scaleFactor`, and both `CoreTextTextSystem.rasterize(_:)` and `PortableTextSystem.rasterize(_:)` honour it, so a third-party conformer breaks nothing. `GlyphAtlas` gains a `package` slot-to-key map, written only on a pack miss and emptied by `reclaim()` (`keysBySlot`, `key(forSlot:)`, `key(forAtlasX:y:width:height:)`), and `packedGlyph(for:)` (a read that neither stamps nor rasterizes). A slot is unique within a `layoutGeneration`, and every holder of a slot already drops on a generation change (`GL-D`), so a stale slot reads `nil`. The atlas holds each scaled glyph beside its base raster (the fallback needs it); an animated zoom adds at most one raster set per rung and `wantsReclaim` frees stale rungs. **A new holder keeps keys, not slots** (text.md).

**Counters** (`Frame`, all frame-local): `glyphsRekeyed`, `glyphRekeyFallbacks`, `glyphRekeyLookups`.

## TZ-B: a clip between nested non-flattening effects is a masked group (divergence 109 retired)

`Frame.paintWithDeviceMap` opens a mask scope around a non-flattening effect scope when `nestedClipMask()` returns a mask: `paintWithMask(primitive:maskDepth:)` with the clip in force as a white rounded rect, emitted in the space outside the new scope, so every enclosing effect maps it on its way out (`RenderEffect.apply` maps a mark's masks, `CM-A`, `CM-F`). The scope's own outer mask stays the screen box, a superset the mask multiplies. The mask rect's content mask is the clip grown by `max(16, size)` on every side (`CM-I`; never `unboundedMask`). `nestedClipMask()` is `nil` (the box stays) when no clip was pushed since the innermost non-flattening effect, when a scope that is not a flattening **effect** (a transition, capture or fade) opened after the clip, when a mask scope already cuts the clip in force (an ellipse or path clip: it would add a second, redundant group), and when the composed map keeps the clip an axis-aligned square rect (a quarter turn: the old arithmetic). A flattening effect between the two is undone (a uniform scale and a translation are always invertible; probe `NC5` is exact). Fallbacks are `CM`'s: a material or glass inside keeps the box (`masksFlattenedBackdrop`), a group over 8192 draws per primitive (`masksFlattenedOversize`). Counter `nestedClipMasks`. Hit testing and accessibility stay the bounding rect (`CM-E`). Probes `NC1`..`NC5`: SwiftUI clips exactly between two rotations (0 ink beyond 1 px; the box would add 2724 pixels), rounded corners cut, under an outer `rotation3DEffect`, for an ellipse, and with a flattening scale between.

## TZ-C: limits and fallbacks

The raster stops at **256 device pixels of em** (about 360 such glyphs fit in a 4096 square atlas) and at **a quarter** of the device size. Past them the raster is resampled by the remainder (`bounds = slot * f / k`). **These limits are MetalUI's, not SwiftUI's** (`Z7`: SwiftUI shows no cap at 20x); they stay in 106's residue. A glyph the atlas refuses at the new key (it is full and at its cap, `GL-E`), or whose slot has no key (the CoreText-only `draw(_ placed: PlacedGlyph)` path, test-only), is drawn from its base raster as emitted and counted in `glyphRekeyFallbacks`; it is never dropped.

## TZ-D: what divergence 106 keeps

Text under a rotation, skew, non-uniform scale or projection, and under a scale that does not flatten, keeps the upright device-scale raster resampled through the transform (`Z3`, `Z6`: SwiftUI is vector or per-axis exact; a per-angle key would blow up the atlas under an animation and is rejected). Text inside a projective filtered group is rendered in creation space and resampled (`GF-B`). A text shadow or blur silhouette is a CPU leaf raster at base resolution (`Blur.swift` reads `glyphAtlas.pixels` at the base slot; the leaf is blurred, so the cost is small). `drawText` surface text. The row's image clause is dropped: SwiftUI samples a bitmap image too, and the clause was never measured.
