# `ViewThatFits` — decisions (prefix `VF-`)

Rulings for the new public `ViewThatFits` and `Axis`, new divergence rows 219 and 220. Branch `feat/view-that-fits` (master `c5c1c09d`). Record 217. Evidence: the probe `docs/probes/swiftui-view-that-fits.swift` (arm ids below; compiled, run three times byte-identical, 2026-11-06, macOS 27.2, Swift 6.4.2, screen locked, no computer use; its header records the output, what is not measured and the readings that differ from the plan). The plan's arms had been run as `swift -` scripts that were not kept: the probe rebuilds them from the plan's tables and keeps their ids. MetalCreator's request is `docs/metalui-gaps.md` TB-a in that repo. Next unused id: `VF-K`.

## VF-A: the rule

1. **SwiftUI** (`H1`, `V1`, `B1`, `H2`, `W1`, `W2`, `Z1`, `E2`, `E3`): the children are tried in order. A child is asked its **ideal** along the named axes (the proposal with those axes made `nil`, the other axis keeping its value: asked `(nil, H)` for `.horizontal`, `(W, nil)` for `.vertical`, `(nil, nil)` for both). It **fits** when, on every named axis, the proposal is `nil` or the ideal is `<=` it (`W = 100` picks the 100-wide child, `W = 60` the 60-wide, `W = 59.99` the 30-wide). The first that fits is chosen and **the last child is the fallback**; children after the chosen one are never asked. The chosen child is then proposed the **whole** proposal and the view takes its answer, even past the proposal (`H1`: 100 x 20 at W = 10; `H2`: the unnamed axis is ignored). The fit test is the ideal alone: an ideal of 50 that answers 150 at W = 100 is chosen (`W1`), an ideal of 150 that would answer 50 is rejected (`W2`). A `ForEach` or `Group` member is its own candidate (`E2`, `E3`). Inside `ScrollView(.horizontal)` the width proposal is `nil`, so the first child is chosen (`S1`).
2. **MetalUI**: `ViewThatFits(in: Axis.Set = [.horizontal, .vertical], @ProposalContentBuilder content:)`, a `ProposalElement`. Legacy content enters through `LegacyContent` like every proposal container's (`PE-C`: a legacy item field in a candidate traps by name). An empty axis set tests nothing and always chooses the first child (SwiftUI's `ViewThatFits(in: [])` is unmeasured).
3. **What the view reports** (`I1`..`I4`): its parent sees the chosen child's answer to the proposal it was given, so a constrained proposal answers the fallback or the fitting child and an unconstrained one the first (`I1`: `(nil,nil)` 100, `(0,0)` 30, `(80,50)` 60). In an `HStack` it is flexible between the last child's answer and the first's, so beside a sibling it is offered only its share (`I4`: 150, 70, 20 at W = 200, 120, 70). `ViewThatFits {}` with no children answers zero (inferred, not probed). `.fixedSize()` reads the first child (`I6`). A nested view counts as its first child, so nesting does not cascade (`N1`).
4. **A flexible candidate is measured at its ideal** (`F1`..`F4`, the app's need 3): a greedy `Color` is **10**, not 0; `HStack(spacing: 0) { 50; Spacer() }` 58 (a `Spacer(minLength: 0)` 50); the drag handle's `{ 50; Color.clear.frame(maxWidth: .infinity, maxHeight: .infinity) }` 60; `Text("Hello wide world")` 100 x 16 and rejected at 70 (it would wrap); `frame(minWidth: 80, maxWidth: .infinity)` 80.
5. **`.layoutPriority`** (`PR1`, `PR2`): a priority on the view works, on its only child it is **not passed through** (`nativeLayoutPriority` answers 0). **The probe file's `PR` arms do not separate** (the plan's setup was not recoverable); the reading is the plan's, pinned at the kernel by `aViewThatFitsPassesNoChildPriority` and carried as unmeasured here.

## VF-B: a kernel case, not a `ProposalLayout`

`NativeNode.firstFitting(horizontal:vertical:)` in `MetalUILayout/LayoutTree.swift`, registered by `package func newNativeFirstFitting(children:horizontal:vertical:)` (no public kernel API; `LayoutPass.requestNativeFirstFitting` is `package` too). A `ProposalLayout` cannot do this: it must place every subview and `ProposalLayoutContainer` prepaints its whole content group. **measureBody** asks each child in order its ideal (`fixedSizeProposal`, cache hits after the first pass), breaks at the first fit and answers `measureNative(chosen, proposal)`; with no children, zero. **placeNative** recomputes the choice at the placement proposal, places only the chosen child in the node's own bounds at the whole proposal (the `.layoutPriority` precedent), records `fitChoices[node] = offset` and leaves the other candidates' subtrees at zero rects, unvisited. Per-case sites: `markSpacers` stops (a `Spacer` below is not an enclosing stack's), `spacingEdges` answers the first child's (`VF-G`), `isCrossAxisSpacer` and `nativeLayoutPriority` the defaults, `nodeSignature` tag 14 with both flags and the children, the grid-cell walk stops. `package func firstFittingChoice(_:) -> Int?` reads the choice back.

## VF-C: elision

The unchosen candidates are **built** (their nodes registered) and then **skipped**: `Element.prepaintGroup` returns **`PrepaintState?`** and answers `nil`, with no prepaint and no bounds record, for an element whose node is in `Frame.elidedNodes` (set by `applyFitChoices()` after layout, before `transitions.afterLayout`); its id goes to `elidedIDs`. `paintGroup` takes the `Optional` and returns on `nil`. `ModifiedContent` and the typed defaults go through that entry with the outermost layer's node. **Every group-level hook that registers before it reaches its content must guard on its own** (found by reading every `prepaintGroup` and by the census): `GeometryChangeScope` (filters its elided nodes), `ModifierKeysScope` (its layout now keeps its nodes), `IdentifiedGroup` and `ForEach`'s scroll target (`Frame.allElided`), and **`AnyElement`'s group entry, a copy of the default that the census found unguarded** (it now checks in both phases). `TransactionScope`, `CoordinateSpaceScope` and `GeometryChangeScope`, whose layered path called `Element.prepaintGroup` statically, return `Content.GroupPrepaint?`. `ListRows` and `Picker`'s option group hold `Optional` states. Guard: `everyCandidateKindRegistersNothingWhenUnchosen` (Text, Button, Toggle, Picker, Menu, TextField, List, Grid, LazyVStack, ScrollView, a Component, an `.equatable()` Component, an `AnyElement`, a `ForEach` element, a legacy `Row`, each against a fallback-only twin: prepaints, paints, scene, accessibility, shortcuts, lifecycle) and `everyGroupLevelHookRegistersNothingWhenUnchosen`. A new element kind or scope that registers in a group-level hook owes an arm.

## VF-D: build-time notes are retracted

Lifecycle entries (`noteLifecycle`) and presentation records (`notePresentation`) are noted **at build**, before the layout that decides the choice. While a view's content is being built (`Frame.fitDepth > 0`) each note is journaled with the node-index interval its scope's content registered (`FitNote`; the three sites of each pass their interval). `applyFitChoices()` merges the intervals of the unchosen candidates (candidate `i` owns `(root[i-1], root[i]]`, a post-order allocation, asserted in debug builds) and **retracts every note whose interval lies inside the merged run**: `LifecycleStore.retract(_:)` removes the entry before the build closes into events (so no `onAppear`, `onChange` or `.task` runs and a previously present scope disappears), and the presentation record leaves `presentationRecords` before anything dispatches. A scope over a `Group { A; B }` is retracted only when neither is chosen. **Not retracted** (divergence 219): the toolbar strip (built before layout), the navigation title, subtitle, document, dismiss behaviour and colour-scheme preference (first writer wins at build), matched-geometry claims, scroll keys, transitions, `noteActiveAnimation` (a spinner or `TimelineView` in an unchosen candidate keeps the display link awake, carried), and the `Deferred` placeholders' presentation roots (laid out, never prepainted).

## VF-E: identity and state

Candidates are positional children under the view's id, built every frame, so **a candidate's `@State` is live and survives a switch** (`R2`: `B` shows 3 after `C` and back, then 4; `stateSurvivesASwitchBetweenCandidates`). SwiftUI creates the state of a candidate it measured and rejected and keeps it across switches (`R2`, `R3`: `A` returns with the storage made at `S0`); the probe **differs from the plan** in that it creates the state of **every** candidate, the ones after the chosen one included (`S0b`), so row 220 is narrower than the plan said. The residue: MetalUI seeds all of them on the first built frame, SwiftUI when it first measures or, as read here, always. A switch is an appearance and a disappearance, **in MetalUI's order** (`LC-F`: appearances first; the probe read `disappear(B) appear(C)`), a difference of the lifecycle rule and not of this view.

## VF-F: focus

A focused element inside a candidate that becomes unchosen **loses focus and does not regain it** when the candidate returns (`FO1`, `FO2`: `@FocusState` false, the responder is the window). `Frame.resolveFocus` clears `focusedElement` when it is, or descends from, an id in `elidedIDs` (the `$focus` slot is still live, so the ordinary retention would have kept it). Pin: `focusInACandidateThatIsNoLongerChosenIsCleared`.

## VF-G: spacing edge and priority

The view presents its **first candidate's** default-spacing edges, whichever is chosen (`SP1`: `{ Text; Color }` has the `Text`'s gap whether the `Text` or the `Color` is chosen; `SP2`: `{ Color (unchosen); Text }` the `Color`'s). `nativeLayoutPriority` is 0 (`PR2`). Pin: `theSpacingEdgeIsTheFirstCandidates`.

## VF-H: memo, graft and replay

1. **The placement memo carries the choices.** `LayoutMemo.Placed` and `TranslatedPlacement` hold `choices: [FitChoice]` (the view's position in the record's pre-order and the offset it chose); a restore (`placeFromMemo`, `subtreePlacement`) writes them back, and the verify mode compares them with a fresh placement. **A translated restore (`RG-E`) shifted every rect of the record by the origin's move, the zero rects of the unvisited candidates included**: found under `METALUI_VERIFY_LAYOUT_MEMO` (the subtree trap named node 0 at x = 10); `restoreFitChoices` re-zeroes the unchosen candidates' subtrees. The plan's stop condition 3 ("choices cannot be restored bit for bit") did not fire: the fix is part of the record, not an exclusion from the memo.
2. **A switch is a different subtree.** `subtreeSignature` and `subtreeSignatures` (the retained key's hash) walk only the **chosen** child of a view and mix the choice, so a retained occurrence that holds a view misses when it switches even with equal rects, and a moved occurrence matches (the unplaced nodes' rects would otherwise move with the origin).
3. **No graft in or around a view.** `BuildAudit.fitContainers` (the spans a window registered) refuses by the name `viewThatFits`; a graft window opened while `fitDepth > 0` is tainted by the same name (a graft replays its lifecycle notes through the store, not `Frame.noteLifecycle`, so inside a candidate they could not be retracted). A guard in `graftRecord(for:)` was written and removed: a graft record is made only by a window closed outside every view, and an occurrence's identity path includes the view's id, so no record can be taken from outside to inside (unreachable, no test can redden it).
4. **An `.equatable()` occurrence whose top nodes are all elided** bypasses replay and recording: `EquatableComponent.prepaintGroup` forwards to the component, whose elements elide themselves, and its paint takes the plain branch. Prepaint and paint replay of an occurrence that is chosen are unchanged (the key includes the choice).

## VF-I: `Axis`

SwiftUI's `Axis` (`.horizontal`, `.vertical`) and `Axis.Set` (`OptionSet`), new in `Axis.swift`. MetalUI had `ScrollAxis` only, which is unchanged. **A module that declares its own `Axis` and imports `MetalUI` sees an ambiguity** at an unqualified use (`docs/migration.md`); MetalCreator's `CreatorGeometry.Axis` is the known case and its build is the check (record 217).

## VF-J: unmeasured, and carried

- SwiftUI's `ViewThatFits {}` answers zero (inferred); a switch is not animated (inferred); an unchosen candidate's `navigationTitle` and toolbar items are ignored (nearest arm: `X0`'s alert; **row 219**); a `Spacer` as a candidate; a custom parent placing the view in larger bounds (the chosen child takes the view's bounds here, as the `.layoutPriority` precedent does).
- An open `Menu` or popover whose row becomes unchosen: the presentation record is retracted, so it is dismissed by the ordinary rule; not separately tested.
- Time-driven work in an unchosen candidate (a spinner, `TimelineView`) keeps the display link awake, so `runUntilIdle` may report not idle.
- Every candidate is built every frame: a 15-row toolbar costs 15 rows of build, and grafts inside or around a view are refused (`VF-H`).
- The `Deferred` presentations of an unchosen candidate are laid out against the window and never prepainted.
- Human group `VF` (`docs/verification/human-checks.md`): MetalCreator's sketch bar across every threshold on macOS, Linux and Windows, each tool key once at every width, VoiceOver and AccessKit seeing only the visible row, a `More` menu open during a resize, the drag handle at every width.
