# Matched geometry and the Transition protocol - decisions

Rulings for `matchedGeometryEffect`, `MatchedGeometryProperties`, the `Transition` protocol (`TransitionPhase`, `TransitionContent`) and what round 3 item R3-2 carried (`contentTransition`, `.blurReplace`, `.modifier(active:identity:)`, the `glassEffectID` morph). Prefix `MG-`. Next unused id: `MG-I`.
Branch `feat/matched-geometry` from master `7d3d1b80` (record 175). SwiftUI's side is `docs/probes/swiftui-matched-geometry.swift` (arms C0, MG1-MG13, S1-S13, P1-P6, CT0-CT4, TP1-TP4; recorded output in its header, `SA-O`); the tests are `Tests/MetalUITests/MatchedGeometryTests.swift` and the guard in `TransitionCompileGuards.swift`.
Why: round 3's list had matched geometry and custom transitions as the two largest "Not offered" absences (`AN-AE`'s unsupported list); `LG-K` (divergence 828) had carried the Liquid Glass morph for want of `matchedGeometryEffect`.

## MG-A - the surface and the mechanism

**Ruling.** (1) `ElementGroup.matchedGeometryEffect(id:in:properties:anchor:isSource:)` returns `MatchedGeometryGroup<Self>`, SwiftUI's signature with `id` any `Hashable`, `properties: MatchedGeometryProperties = .frame` (`.position`, `.size`, `.frame`), `anchor: UnitPoint = .center`, `isSource: Bool = true`. `Namespace` is the existing `@Namespace` (`LG-K`), unchanged. (2) The group is **identity- and layout-transparent**, `TransitionGroup`'s shape: it forwards `parent` and `cursor`, so adding it moves no id and resets no `@State`; it is an `Element` over an `Element` and a `StyledElement` over a `StyledElement` (`ES-E`'s shape), and has the typed `ProposalElementGroup` entry as a line-for-line copy. (3) Its bookkeeping is the window-owned `MatchedGeometryStore` in `AnimationStore.matched`, **never a `StateTable` slot** (the seven slots stay seven), keyed by the position the group sits at (`$matched` under `(parent, cursor)`). (4) The effect is **paint only**: `afterLayout` computes, per group, the rectangle it is laid out at and the rectangle it is drawn at, and `paintGroup` opens an affine render-effect scope mapping the first to the second (`Frame.paintWithDeviceMap`, the part of `paintWithRenderEffect` after it has resolved its effect: flattened on the CPU for a translation and a uniform scale, a transform record otherwise).
**Why not relayout.** A relayout at the shown size would move siblings and need the proposal kernel to animate a node across parents; the paint map reuses `GX-G` whole and is the shape a ghost and a drag preview already replay.
**Pins.** `aNewcomerFliesFromTheLeaversFrameToItsOwn`, `theModifierMovesNothingAtRest`, `aFlightKeepsTheDisplayLinkAwakeThenLeavesNothing`. Mutations: M-A, M-I, M-J.

## MG-B - the handoff

**Ruling.** A record new this frame (its position was not claimed last frame) **takes the frame a source record of the same namespace and id drew last frame**, when that record's position is no longer claimed, **under the animation in effect at the new group** (the transaction, the lexical slot as a fallback: `conditionalAnimation`, `AN-AI` item 4), and starts a **flight** from there to its own frame, linear in the animation's value, in position and size for `.frame`. No animation, no flight (MG1n). Measured (probe S1, S3, S11): the newcomer is drawn at the leaver's frame on the first frame of the change and lands on its own; the recorder reads the geometry delta `[325, 155, 50, 30]` = B − A in centre and size.
**Which leavers hand over.** (a) A leaver removed at once by an **explicit** `.transition(.identity)` (or any transition with nothing to play on removal) hands **nothing** over (S2, S4-S8): SwiftUI keeps no frame to take from a view it removes in the same frame. A view with **no** `.transition` hands its frame over, though MetalUI removes it instantly where SwiftUI cross-fades it (divergence 98): the idiom `if a { A.matchedGeometryEffect } else { B.matchedGeometryEffect }` must work as written. (b) A leaver that is **not a source** hands nothing over (S12, S13). (c) The leaver must have been **drawn** last frame (a record with a `displayed` rectangle); a position not claimed because its conditional was not evaluated (a `List` row out of its window, `TB-AH`) is read as a leaver all the same - the only false handoff, and it needs a second view with the same id arriving in the same frame.
**Interruption.** A leaver's handed-over frame is where it was **drawn** last frame, flight included, so a swap back mid-flight starts where the view was (MetalUI's own rule, unprobed; test `aFlightInterruptedByAnotherSwapStartsWhereTheViewWasDrawn`). A flight lives only while its record is claimed; a landed flight is dropped at its last paint.
**Pins.** `withoutATransactionTheNewcomerIsAtItsOwnFrameAtOnce`, `aLeaverRemovedByAnIdentityTransitionHandsNothingOver`, `onlyAMatchingSourceLeaverHandsItsFrameOver`, `aFlightInterruptedByAnotherSwapStartsWhereTheViewWasDrawn`. Mutations: M-B, M-E, M-F, M-F2, M-K.

## MG-C - sources, followers and `properties`

**Ruling.** Of the live records with one `(namespace, id)` the **last source in tree order** is the source (P1: both sources, A is drawn at B's frame, hidden under it); every other record is a **follower** drawn at the source's frame as its own `properties` select (P2, P3, P5), at rest and not only in a swap. With **no source** nothing is shared (P4, S13). `.position` takes the source's position and keeps the view's own size (S9: the newcomer starts with the leaver's centre and its own size; P5); `.size` keeps the view's position and takes the source's size; `.frame` takes both. A view with an empty set shares nothing.
**Unmeasured.** SwiftUI's `.size` alone showed no visible motion in arms S10 and P6 (a fixed-frame child keeps its size whatever it is proposed); MetalUI implements the name, so a `.size` handoff scales the drawn content. Recorded in the probe header as unresolved.
**Pins.** `propertiesSelectWhichPartOfTheFrameIsHandedOver`, `aFollowerIsDrawnAtTheSourcesFrame`. Mutations: M-C, M-G.

## MG-D - `anchor`

**Ruling.** `anchor` names the point of each rectangle put on the other's when `.position` is shared: the view is drawn so that its `anchor` point lies on the source's `anchor` point (the centre by default, S9/P5). For `.size` alone it names the point kept fixed. SwiftUI's `anchor` is **unprobed** (the recorder sees the geometry as a centre-based pair and the arms use the default); this is MetalUI's reading of the documented "anchor point used to produce the frame".
**Pin.** `anAnchorPicksTheCornerThatIsHandedOver`. Mutation: M-D.

## MG-E - what stays at the laid-out frame, what is not drawn

**Ruling.** (1) **Hit testing, focus and accessibility use the laid-out geometry** while a view flies or follows (divergence 895), as an inserting transition's do (`AN-AE` item 5): the group registers nothing. (2) The mapped content is **stretched**, not relaid out, when the size changes - glyphs resample, corner radii and borders scale with the horizontal factor (divergence 896). (3) The **leaver is not moved toward the newcomer**: SwiftUI moves the outgoing view to the newcomer's frame as it fades (S1, S12); MetalUI draws its ghost (if it has a removal transition) where it was (divergence 897). The newcomer alone flies, which is the visible morph when the leaver fades.
**Pin.** `aFlyingViewIsHitAtItsFinalFrame` (1). Mutation: none cheap - the group's `prepaintGroup` is a pure forward; the pin is red only if a registration site is added there.

## MG-F - the `Transition` protocol

**Ruling.** `public protocol Transition: Sendable { func body(content: TransitionContent, phase: TransitionPhase) -> TransitionContent }`, `TransitionPhase` (`.willAppear`, `.identity`, `.didDisappear`, `isIdentity`, `value` -1/0/1), `AnyTransition(_:)` over a transition and `.transition(_:)` overloaded for it. **`body` returns `TransitionContent`**, a value recording `opacity`, `scaleEffect` (uniform, with an anchor) and `offset`, composed in call order (opacities multiply, scales multiply, offsets add) - not a view (divergence 898): a transition is a paint effect here, `TransitionAtom`s moving between two states. The state at `.willAppear` is where an insertion starts, `.didDisappear` where a removal ends, `.identity` rest (TP1, TP2); the atoms are the active state **relative to** the identity state's (`alpha`, `scale` and offset ratios and differences), so a transition whose identity is not the untouched content still moves between the right values. Reduce Motion turns a custom transition into an opacity fade like every other (`AN-AD`, R5). `TransitionContent.init` is internal (plain-import guard `theUnsupportedTransitionsDoNotCompile`). `body` runs three times per resolution (once per phase), with no caching: write it as a pure function of `phase`.
**Pins.** `aCustomTransitionInsertsFromItsWillAppearState`, `aCustomTransitionRemovesToItsDidDisappearState`, `aCustomTransitionIsAskedForEveryPhase`, `reduceMotionTurnsACustomTransitionIntoAFade`, the guard. Mutations: M-L, M-M, M-N, M-O, M-F2 (guard).

## MG-G - retained replay

**Ruling.** An occurrence whose build claimed a matched group is **graft-ineligible by name** (`BuildAudit.matched` -> `"matchedGeometry"`, the `transitions` channel's shape): the claims are not journaled, and a grafted occurrence would claim nothing, turning every follower into a snap and the next frame's newcomer into a false handoff. The store's flights are part of `AnimationStore.Checkpoint` (a redo returns them), its claims are cleared by `rollbackBuild`. `METALUI_VERIFY_RETAINED=1` passes over the whole suite.
**Pin.** `aFlightInsideRetainedOccurrencesLeavesTheStillSiblingAlone` (also under the oracle). Mutation: M-P.

## MG-H - carried

**Ruling.** Not built in this item, each ruled absent (no inert declarations): **`contentTransition(_:)`** (probe CT0-CT4: `.identity` is the only one that suppresses the text cross-fade; `.opacity`, `.interpolate` and `.numericText()` all record the same opacity delta and the same frame change; MetalUI draws a changed string at once and has no old-text capture to cross-fade with - a text ghost needs the `TextRuns` of the previous string kept per `Text` identity, and `.numericText()` per-digit glyph interpolation on top of it); **`.blurReplace`** (TP3: opacity, a blur radius `7 -> 0` and a 0.1 scale - the transition scope has no blur atom; `LK-K`'s blur is a leaf scope, not an effect); **`.modifier(active:identity:)`** (needs `ViewModifier`; the `Transition` protocol covers its use); **the `glassEffectID` morph** (divergence 828 stays): `glassEffectID` could register a matched group, but a glass blob is merged at paint from member rects (`LG-G`), so a morphing member needs the container to merge on **shown** rects and the flight to scale a backdrop record's corner radii non-uniformly; content that carries glass already flies when written with `matchedGeometryEffect` itself. **`AnyTransition.animation(_:)`** stays carried (`AN-AE`).
**Guard.** `theUnsupportedTransitionsDoNotCompile` keeps `.blurReplace` and `contentTransition` absent.
