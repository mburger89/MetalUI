# Group opacity through an offscreen group pass — decisions (prefix `GO-`)

Rulings for R4-5 PR A: an opacity written outside `compositingGroup()` composites the group through one offscreen pass on both renderers (**divergence 205 retired**; 221 and 222 added). Branch `feat/group-opacity` (master `3c7eadb9`). Record 218. Evidence: the probe `docs/probes/swiftui-group-opacity.swift` (arms `GO0`..`GO7`; compiled, run twice byte-identical, 2026-11-06, macOS 27.2, Swift 6.4.2, screen locked, no computer use; its header records the output). Next unused id: `GO-I`. The plan's other PRs (B: a GPU shadow and blur of the group's texture; C: masked group composite; D: gradient `foregroundStyle` on text; E: text re-rasterised at the effective scale; F: continuous-corner SDF) are not built here.

## GO-A: what composites

1. **SwiftUI** (`GO0`..`GO4`, `GO7`): `.compositingGroup().opacity(0.5)` over two overlapping squares reads the top colour over the backdrop, (255, 128, 128) at the overlap; a plain `.opacity(0.5)` on the same container is per leaf, (191, 64, 128) (`GO0`, the separating pair; `GO1`: an opacity written *inside* the group is per leaf). Two opacities on one group multiply (`GO2`, 0.25: 255, 191, 191); the group composites under a rotation (`GO3`) and a group in a group composites twice (`GO4`); `.drawingGroup().opacity` composites too (`GO7`, informational; `drawingGroup()` stays unoffered). `GO8` (a material in a group) cannot be measured: `ImageRenderer` draws no material, so divergence 222 rests on `BB-A`.
2. **MetalUI**: `Frame.paintWithCompositingGroup` pushes its composite scope when a shadow or blur reads it (`PF-E`) **or** when the opacity in force on the group is below 1 and above 0: the entry opacity (`activeOpacity`) times the alpha of every `.transition`, `.fade` and `.effect` scope outside it, up to the last `Deferred` barrier (`compositeReaders`). At opacity 1, at 0 and with no group nothing is pushed and every byte is today's (CG4; `aGroupAloneOrAtFullOpacityChangesNothing`).
3. An opacity group's content is painted **unfaded** (`Frame.withOpacityBase` empties the opacity stack for the body; an `.opacity` written inside re-fills it, which is `GO1`). A group that holds a `.backdrop` or a `.surface` is **not** marked: the entry opacity multiplies each primitive (a backdrop record's `opacity`, a surface quad's) and the live backdrop stays live (divergence 222; a surface's target is resolved from the frame's own scene by both renderers, a group's is not). A `Deferred` inside is not in the group and, drawing under the cleared base, is not faded by it (`RenderEffects.withoutRenderEffects` now pushes its barrier for a composite scope too).

## GO-B: marks and tokens

`CapturedPrimitive.groups: [GroupMark]`, **outermost first**, no new `Kind`. `GroupMark` is a `GroupToken` (a final class: identity, so a replayed leaf keeps the token it was recorded with and never merges with a freshly painted group) and an alpha. A composite scope adds one mark at index 0 of every leaf as the leaf leaves it. An enclosing transition, fade or effect multiplies `groups[0].alpha`, never the colours (`CapturedPrimitive.multiplyAlpha`, the flattening branch of `RenderEffect.apply`); `bakeGroupAlpha()` multiplies every mark into the colours (the belt of `insertIntoScene`, a single leaf, an oversize group, a CPU raster's input).

## GO-C: the insertion walk and its limits

`Frame.insertLeaves(_:)` is **the one place paint leaves reach the scene**: the bottom of `processScopes`, `replayPaint`'s fast path and a ghost's draw go through it, **a new paint path that inserts leaves goes through it**, never `insertIntoScene` (which bakes a mark away: drag previews keep today's look). Each run of contiguous primitives whose outermost mark is one token (and one alpha) drops that mark and is drawn inline at alpha >= 1, dropped at <= 0, as one faded primitive when it is a single one with no deeper mark (`groupsBaked`), and otherwise rendered into a `Scene` of its own that the main scene holds as one quad (`groupsComposited`). The box is the union of what the inner scene paints (`Scene.paintedBounds()`: bounds mapped by the transform's bounding box, cut by the content mask), grown one pixel for anti-aliasing, floored and ceiled without libm, cut by the target; a side above 8192 draws the group per primitive (`groupsFlattenedOversize`). The quad is a `MUIImage` over the box, `ImageFilter.nearest`, `opacity = alpha`, mask = the box. `shadowItem` and `blurItem` take baked leaves (divergence 221).

## GO-D: `PrimitiveKind.group`

`MUIImage` quads in `Scene.groups`, one `Scene` each in `Scene.groupScenes` (finalized, may hold groups; **coordinates are the parent's**, the target is offset by the quad's box origin). `insert(_:group:layer:)`, `isEmpty`, `clear()`, `finalize()` (a group is its own run), `emissionSequence`, `layer`, `highestLayer`, `primitives(inSequences:)` all read the new arrays. **Public break** (as `BB-L`): an exhaustive `switch` over `PrimitiveKind` outside the package adds a `.group` arm (`docs/migration.md`).

## GO-E: the Metal pass

`Renderer.encodeGroups` runs at the top of `encode`, post-order, each group into a target from the window's `BackdropFrameState` pool (released when the frame's encode ends, so a steady frame makes none; `lastGroupPasses`, `lastGroupTexturesMade`, `lastGroupAllocationFailures`, test hook `groupPoolFailsAllocation`). The group pass is the old single pass (`encodeSinglePass`) into the target with `loadAction .clear` transparent, the view's viewport the box size and a **post-NDC translation** `(-2 ox / w, +2 oy / h)` as the projection: exact because every fragment reads its position, clip and SDF from the interpolated pre-projection point (`pixelPosition`), never `[[position]]` (checked: only the blur and copy pipelines read it), and a projective clip stays linear in `w`. `shaders.metal` is unchanged. A group scene's own backdrops draw their flat tint (it holds none by `GO-A`). Image textures are prepared for the whole tree (a nested scene's textures are not released by the root's prepare). An allocation failure draws the group as nothing and counts it (the `BB-I` precedent).

## GO-F: SDL and the fixture

`mui_renderer_finish` and `replay_render` take `ReplayGroup` descriptors and a main-run count. The bridge uploads one buffer per kind, so the groups' records follow the root's (`SDLGroupSet` in `MetalUISDL`, `FlatFrame` in `ReplayFixture`: transform indices shifted by the transforms before them, run starts by the records before them, group quads naming `base + index` in the handle array), and `render_groups` draws each into a pool slot of `BackdropState` between the atlas copy pass and the frame's passes (the atlas must be uploaded first: a group may hold glyphs). `backdrop_begin_frame` moved to the two callers; an unused-slot release replaces `release_all` on the single-pass path so a group's slot survives it. `replay.hlsl` is unchanged (blobs and `SOURCE.sha256` do not move). **`ReplayFixture` is version 7** (versions 2-6 load): after the runs, `u32 groupCount`, per group in post-order `u32 x, y, width, height` and a nested body (rects, glyphs, images, transforms, runs); `FixtureRun.Kind.group = 4` names group quads in an image array. **Parity frame 16** (`Experiments/SDLGPU`, `--expect 17`): an overlap group, a group in a group, a rotated group and a group of a word and a gradient raster; 0 differing pixels on SDL-over-MSL (record 218). `MetalUISDL` cannot import `ReplayFixture` (the root manifest does not declare it), so the flattening exists twice, pinned by the same frames.

## GO-G: retained and ghosts

An `.equatable()` occurrence's recorded leaves carry their marks (values; `RetainedTranslation` moves each child); `replayPaint` inserts through `insertLeaves`, so a replay composites again (`aRetainedReplayOfAnOpacityGroupStillComposites`). The composite scope was already in `retainedAmbientKey`; inside the group `activeOpacity` reads 1, so an outer opacity change does not touch an inner record. A default ghost (`XF-H`) of a unit holding a group expands the group's scene into captured primitives under one fresh mark at the quad's opacity (`Scene.capturedGroup(at:)`), so the fade multiplies that mark. A unit whose paint sits **inside** an open opacity group notes an empty range (a composite scope collects and re-emits: the existing rule), so it ghosts nothing.

## GO-H: divergences 221 and 222

**221** (owner R4-5 PR B): a `.shadow` or `.blur` outside an opacity group sees the group's leaves faded one by one, not the composite (`GO5`, `GO6`). **222**: a group holding a material, glass or GPU surface is not composited. Both pinned wrong on purpose.

## Carried

No cross-frame group texture cache (a group re-renders each frame). A unit inside an open opacity group gets no default ghost. `Deferred` content inside a group is no longer faded by the group's opacity (it was per primitive before; SwiftUI's presentations are not faded by their anchor's opacity, unmeasured here). Human group `GO` (`docs/verification/human-checks.md`): the looks demo on Metal, Linux Vulkan and Windows D3D12, fade smoothness, and `W1`/`W2` (the `GO0` pair in a real window against SwiftUI) which the probe could not take with the screen locked.
