# Hierarchical lists (`OutlineGroup`, `List(_:children:)`, `DisclosureGroup`) - decisions (prefix `OG-`)

Rulings for Alloy's project tree: SwiftUI's `DisclosureGroup`, `OutlineGroup` and the outline forms of `List`, built as the design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-outline-disclosure-design.md` lays out (lanes 0-8). Record 309 (lane 0, the probes). **Every ruling below is PROVISIONAL**: it records what the lane-0 probes measured and what the design proposes to do about it; the lane that builds it confirms or amends it, and moves the ruling's status here. Next unused id: `OG-K`. **Lane 1 (record 311, `DisclosureGroup` in eager contexts) finalised `OG-A` (its `DisclosureGroup` part), `OG-D` (its `DisclosureGroup` storage), `OG-H` (its eager animation part) and `OG-J` (its chevron look); the rest of each ruling, and all of `OG-B`, `OG-C`, `OG-E`, `OG-G`, `OG-I`, stay provisional; `OG-F`'s standalone-group accessibility is MetalUI's choice, below.**

Probes: `docs/probes/swiftui-outline.swift` (arms `P-L1`..`P-R1`, output in `docs/probes/swiftui-outline.recorded.txt`) and `docs/probes/swiftui-disclosure-group.swift` (arms `D1`..`D4`, `P-A3`). macOS 27.2.1, Swift 6.4.2, screen unlocked, 2026-11-15. Both files carry a RECORDED header with the values quoted below; where an arm could not be measured the ruling says so and names what stands in for it. Alloy's binding answers (design section 9a): `.sidebar` look, the content form (`List { ForEach { DisclosureGroup(isExpanded:) } }`), single selection, ids unique tree-wide, ⌥ cascade one level per frame without a cap, collapse-with-hidden-selection and expanded-folder-scroll as SwiftUI does per the probe, persist expansion only; the reveal request (setting ancestors' `isExpanded` and `scrollTo` in one transaction must land the row in view) and the lane order 0-6, 8, 7.

## OG-A: the API (provisional; the `DisclosureGroup` part FINAL, record 311)

Class `A`, SwiftUI's spellings and generics exactly as design section 1: `DisclosureGroup` (the `Label: Element, Content` forms, `Label == Text` with `LocalizedStringKey` and `StringProtocol`, with and without `isExpanded:`; content `@escaping`, evaluated only when expanded), `OutlineGroup` (five generics, four inits), the content `List(selection:content:)` and the outline `List` inits, `contextMenu(forSelectionType:menu:primaryAction:)`. Not offered (class `R`): `DisclosureGroupStyle`, Table outlines, `.listStyle`, `Section` in a `List`. **As landed (lane 1):** `DisclosureGroup<Label: ElementGroup, Content: ElementGroup>`, an `ElementGroup` (`Sources/MetalUI/DisclosureGroup.swift`) over an internal `Component` body, with `init(content:label:)`, `init(isExpanded:content:label:)` and the `Label == Text` forms `init(_:content:)`, `init(_:isExpanded:content:)` over `LocalizedStringKey` and over `StringProtocol` (`@_disfavoredOverload`). **The generics are `ElementGroup`, one step wider than the design's `Label: Element`** (the repo's controls take `ElementGroup` labels, as `Toggle` does; every `Element` is one, so SwiftUI source compiles unchanged). `content` is `@escaping` and called only while expanded (`aCollapsedGroupNeverCallsItsContent`: 0 calls over three collapsed frames, 1 per expanded frame). `LocalizedStringResource` forms are class `R`. Probe backing: `swiftui-disclosure-group.swift` D2 (the `String`, `LocalizedStringKey` and literal title forms measure identically: 260 x 24 collapsed, label ink at x 32.5 in a 20-padded window; a `Label` label differs only by its icon, 34), D4 (an uncontrolled group is collapsed initially; `.constant(true)` is expanded at once).

## OG-B: flattening, identity and tree-wide ids (provisional)

Design section 2 stands: one engine, nested named scopes per depth, selection and scroll ids are the element ids, so ids must be unique across the whole tree (Alloy: repo-relative paths). **Moving a node to another parent gives it a new identity**, as SwiftUI: probe `P-I1` (X moved from A to M with the same id: "disappear X" then "appear X" in one turn and the row's `@State` is NEW, born# 3 -> 6, while the unmoved sibling Y keeps 4 and the unrelated N keeps 5; the same in `List(_:children:)`, `List { OutlineGroup }` and the recursive `DisclosureGroup(isExpanded:)`). The divergence label that records tree-wide unique ids is reserved (1085-1094, see the decision-prefixes row).

Extends `LZ-B`: a Component whose content flattens, and the taken branch of an if/else, stay lazy inside a tree. Not probed (a MetalUI-internal rule with no SwiftUI observable); lane 2 pins it.

## OG-D: the expansion model, retention, cascade and reveal (provisional; the `DisclosureGroup` storage FINAL, record 311)

- **Controlled `isExpanded:`** is read during the flatten and written only from input (chevron, keys, AX, ⌥ cascade). **As landed for a standalone group (lane 1):** the binding is read when the body is built and written by the label row's click, Space (while focused) and an accessibility press, each once, as the negation; never from layout or paint (`aControlledBindingIsWrittenOnlyByTheClickAndAProgrammaticWriteRerenders`: 0 writes over four frames, 1 per click, a programmatic write re-renders and is not written back).
- **Uncontrolled** `DisclosureGroup` is a state slot, collapsed initially (`D4`). **As landed:** the body `Component`'s `@State` (`$state0` under the group's own id; no new reserved slot name, so `theSevenRetentionSlotsAreMutuallyDistinct` is unmoved); it persists across frames and, as any `@State`, resets if the group's identity moves (`OG-B`). **Uncontrolled outlines retain descendant expansion**: probe `P-E5` (expand A, expand A/B, collapse A, re-expand A: A/B is still expanded, `A+ | A/B+ | A/B/D | A/B/E | A/C | Z | M-`) in `List(_:children:)`, `List { OutlineGroup }` and a content-form `DisclosureGroup` with no `isExpanded:`. One slot per subgroup, as design section 2; the slot survives the ancestor's collapse.
- **⌥ cascade (`P-K4`, `P-K5`, `P-C1`).** In SwiftUI, ⌥→ on a collapsed group expands it and ALL descendants at once, and in the content form SwiftUI writes every descendant's `isExpanded` binding in the SAME run-loop turn, including the bindings of rows that had never been built (`K4d`: a 5-deep chain, four bindings written in one turn). ⌥← collapses the group and every descendant (two bindings in one turn); ⌥← on A/B leaves its parent open. ⌥-click on the chevron of a collapsed group expands it and its descendants at once (`C1c`). ⌥→ on an ALREADY expanded group does nothing (`K4b`: descendants stay collapsed). Alloy accepts MetalUI doing one level per frame with no cap (design section 3, 9a.4; divergence reserved): SwiftUI's same-turn cascade writes bindings of rows it has not built; MetalUI's lazy engine cannot know an unbuilt row's binding, so the cascade there is one level per frame and ends after the depth. That is a MetalUI engine constraint, not something the probe measured in SwiftUI.
- **Collapsing an ancestor of the selection (`P-K7`, Alloy 9a.5).** SwiftUI keeps the hidden id in the binding (no write: `selection` stays `A/B/D` after a chevron click or a programmatic collapse of A, and after collapsing A/B under a selection A/B/D) while the outline has NO selected row; the next arrow key starts from nothing (↓ selects the FIRST row, ↑ the LAST: `K7e`, `K7f`); a collapse by key on the selected group keeps it selected and ↓ moves to the next visible row (`K7d`). MetalUI follows this exactly: the controller keeps the id, shows no selection while it is hidden, and ↑↓ with no visible selection go to the first or last row.
- **Reveal (`P-R1`, Alloy request a).** In ONE transaction (insert the ancestors in the expanded set, set the selection, `proxy.scrollTo(id)` with the default, `.center` or `.top` anchor) SwiftUI does NOT land the row in view, in the same update or in the following second (row 20 stays at y 650 of a 400 high view, `scrollY` 0, 60 frames sampled); `scrollTo` on the next main-queue turn (0 ms) also fails; `scrollTo` 20, 60 and 120 ms later (one frame or more) lands it at y 368 of 400 (`scrollY` 282, the row's bottom edge on the view's bottom edge: the nearest alignment of the default anchor, since the row was below the viewport). The controls (`R1d` expand, wait 6 turns, scroll; `R1e` already expanded and off-screen, `scrollTo` at once: y 368, `scrollY` 4954) land at the same place. So the requester's "must land the row in view" is STRONGER than SwiftUI: MetalUI is asked to land it in the same transaction. Ruling: the lane that builds reveal makes a `scrollTo` of an id that the same transaction made visible resolve against the post-expansion flatten (the expansion is applied to the index first, then the scroll target is resolved), and the test asserts the row in view the same frame, not "as SwiftUI"; a divergence (a stronger guarantee than SwiftUI's) is reserved for it. The nearest alignment is the default landing (bottom edge for a row below the viewport); `.center` and `.top` were measured only in the failing arm, so their landing positions are unknown and follow the existing `scrollTo` anchors.
- **Same-transaction expand + `scrollTo` (Alloy, 2026-11-15, accepted).** Alloy accepted that MetalUI resolves a `scrollTo` made in the same transaction as the expansion against that frame's post-expansion layout, so the row lands in view in the same frame, **unlike SwiftUI**, which needs a frame in between (`P-R1`). The labelled divergence (a stronger guarantee than SwiftUI's) is added by the lane that implements it (lane 4 or 5) from the reserved 1085-1094; lane 1 adds none.
- **Expanded-folder scroll (Alloy 9a.7).** Expanding does not scroll (`N1`: rows below slide, nothing scrolls); a newly expanded folder is not scrolled into view.

## OG-E: keys, selection and focus (provisional)

List is the single focusable; rows are not (`FC-F`). Selection extracts to a `ListSelectionController<ID>` shared by both forms (design section 3). Measured key behaviour, identical in the three forms (`P-K1`..`P-K7`), is the spec; unless a lane finds a reason, MetalUI matches it:

| Key | On | SwiftUI (macOS 27.2.1) |
|---|---|---|
| ↓ ↑ | any | one VISIBLE row per key, one binding write per key, one turn apart (K1) |
| → | collapsed group | expands, selection stays (K2a) |
| → | expanded group | nothing; does NOT move to the first child (K2b) |
| → | leaf | nothing (K2c) |
| ← | expanded group | collapses, selection stays (K3a) |
| ← | collapsed root | nothing (K3b) |
| ← | leaf child | selection to the parent (K3c) |
| ← | collapsed child group | selection to the parent (K3d) |
| ← | expanded child group | collapses it, selection stays (K3e) |
| ⌥→ | collapsed group | expands the group and every descendant (K4a, K4d) |
| ⌥→ | expanded group | nothing (K4b) |
| ⌥← | expanded group | collapses it and every descendant (K5, K5b) |

The design draft's "→ expanded: first child if SwiftUI does that" is settled: SwiftUI does not. **The chevron click does not select** (`P-C1`: nothing selected stays nil, selection Z stays Z, the toggle is the only write); a plain click on the row body selects and does not toggle; ⌥-click on the chevron cascades. The key moves reveal the lead (the AppKit lead is the selected row). Out of scope (Alloy 9a.6): type-select, drag and drop, rename, multi-select.

## OG-F: accessibility (provisional; the standalone group is MetalUI's choice, record 311)

Probe `P-A1`/`P-A2` (the AppKit elements, read in process through the legacy attribute API; identical in the three forms): outline role `AXOutline`; row role `AXRow`, subrole `AXOutlineRow`, role description "outline row"; `AXDisclosureLevel` = depth (0, 1, 2; read-only); `AXDisclosing` 1 or 0 on a group and settable (setting it true on a collapsed row expanded it and wrote the binding: so the "expand" AX action is the `AXDisclosing` write), 0 and read-only on a leaf; `AXDisclosedByRow` = the parent row (nil for a root); `AXDisclosedRows` = the direct children rows (a leaf: none); `AXSelected` settable. **`AXExpanded` is not an attribute of an outline row at all.** The row's only child is an `AXCell` whose only child is an `AXDisclosureTriangle` (`AXValue` 1 expanded, 0 collapsed, action `AXPress`, which collapsed A and wrote `isExpanded[A] = false`). So the neutral model's "expanded" maps to `AXDisclosing` on macOS rows, and the disclosure triangle is a child (design section 4's "triangle child if probe P-A shows one" is settled: yes). **Lane 1 as landed (a standalone group, MetalUI's choice: `P-A3` is unmeasured, so nothing here is SwiftUI's):** the label row publishes as `AccessibilityRole.disclosureTriangle` (AppKit `AXDisclosureTriangle`; SDL/AccessKit `BUTTON`, the `accesskit.h` header is not fetched on the lane's machine so no new C name was used, and the AccessKit `expanded` state is left to lane 5), through a role hint (`AXNode.roleHint == .disclosureTriangle`, stripped like the other role hints), with the label's text as its label (the chevron is `accessibilityHidden`: unhidden, its symbol label "Forward" folded in), value `"1"` expanded and `"0"` collapsed (the value the outline probe read on the real triangle), focusable, press toggling it and refused when disabled. **Not measured:** SwiftUI's own AX elements (the label inside a cell; a standalone `DisclosureGroup`, `P-A3`): the in-process tree is one empty `AXUnknown` group, and an `AXUIElement` client of this process answered only with application-role elements in this environment. The standalone group's role, value, label and press are therefore taken from the outline's: a group row with a disclosure triangle child; a lane (1 or 5) re-measures with a live AX client before it ships the standalone group's AX. SDL/AccessKit: `TREE`/`TREE_ITEM` with expanded, level and EXPAND/COLLAPSE (design section 4; names verified in lane 5).

## OG-G: `contextMenu(forSelectionType:menu:primaryAction:)` (provisional)

Probe `P-M1`..`P-M4` (identical in the three forms; menu built through `menu(for:)`): a menu built on an UNSELECTED row receives `{that row}` and does NOT change the selection (Z stays selected, `M1`); on the selected row `{that row}` (`M1b`); on empty space `{}` (an empty set) and the menu is still returned (`M2`); double-click: the first click selects (one binding write), `primaryAction` follows ~60 ms later with `{row}` (`M3`), and on a group row it does NOT toggle the group (`M3b`); Return with a selection: `primaryAction` with `{selection}`, one turn after the key (`M4`, and `M4b` on a group, no toggle). Design section 3's rule stands (`row in selection ? selection : [row]`; empty space `[]`), simplified by single selection: the closure gets `{clicked row}`, or `{}`. **Not measured:** a real right-click popup and whether a real `rightMouseDown` changes the selection (a modal menu loop; the menus were built through `menu(for:)`, which does not select). MetalUI adds `ClickDispatch.clickCount` (lane 6); a double-click is the second click of one row within the platform interval. Return needs a focused list with a selection.

## OG-H: animation (provisional; the eager part FINAL, record 311)

Probe `P-N1`..`P-N4` (the row below an expanding group; presentation-layer y sampled every 7 ms): a chevron click slides the rows from y 42 to 106 over about 245 ms (15 -> 259 ms data form, 16 -> 254 ms content form), 32 distinct positions, an ease-in-out shape; the chevron glyph swaps (state 0 -> 1) and is NOT a rotated layer (transform identity throughout), so design section 5's "chevron rotates 0 -> 90" is wrong for macOS: the glyph is replaced, collapsed 5 x 8 pt pointing right, expanded 8.5 x 5 pointing down (`P-L3`). A bare `isExpanded = true` SNAPS (one value). `withAnimation(.easeInOut(duration: 0.6)) { isExpanded = true }` slides over the same ~245 ms (15 -> 262 ms), `.linear(duration: 0.6)` around a click the same (16 -> 261 ms): the SwiftUI animation's duration and curve are ignored, AppKit's row animation runs. A bare `NSOutlineView.expandItem` also slides (16 -> 255 ms): the AppKit default is animated, a bare SwiftUI write is what snaps. Whether the glyph cross-fades is unsampled. Ruling: in the lazy list a toggle by input (chevron, keys, AX) slides the rows below by the inserted height over 245 ms with the ease-in-out curve, regardless of the ambient animation; a bare binding write snaps; the standalone group animates by the ambient animation (`swiftui-disclosure-group.swift` D3: a bare write snaps, `withAnimation(.easeInOut(duration: 0.5))` takes 503 ms, 33 positions). The splice animation (rows after the splice slide, inserted rows fade in, removed rows ghost from the previous scene, realised rows only, Reduce Motion cross-fades) is the MetalUI construction of this (lane 7); the 245 ms is the measured duration to match. Order: lane 7 after lane 8 (Alloy request b). **Lane 1, eager contexts, as landed:** the group's content is built under a builder `if`, so a binding write is a `XF-H` default unit: a bare write snaps (alpha 1, nothing in flight) and the same write under `withAnimation(.linear(duration: 1.2))` fades the content in 0 -> 0.33 at 0.4 -> 1 (`aBareWriteSnapsAndAnAnimatedWriteFadesTheContentIn`, the ramp of `XF2`), the removal fading as a ghost; whether the rows below slide with the insertion (SwiftUI's `D3b` series does) is not asserted by lane 1. **A click writes bare, inside no `withAnimation`**: `D3c` is unmeasured, so by this ruling a click snaps (SwiftUI's click animation, if it has one, is not reproduced; no divergence label is added for a behaviour nobody measured). The default click animation of a standalone `DisclosureGroup` (`D3c`) is not measured (a synthetic mouse event does not reach SwiftUI's gesture).

## OG-I: performance (provisional)

Counts, never times, as design section 6: a collapsed subtree costs 0 content evaluations and 0 `children` reads; a warm scroll frame's realised units and layout work are identical at 500 and 100k outline rows; the data form validates expanded groups + the window and rebuilds 0 times on a scroll frame; expanding a 10k-child folder rebuilds once that frame, zero the next, realised = window; **after lane 8, an idle frame (no scroll, no model change) does zero outline evaluation, and Alloy's steady-frame guard (`ViewRerunTests`) must not rise; lane 8 owes the count test** (Alloy 9a.1). Gated: `METALUI_RUN_100K_OUTLINE_TEST=1 swift test --filter anOutlinesWorkIsTheSameFor100kPathsAsFor500`; demo `METALUI_OUTLINE_DEMO=1`. Not probed.

## OG-J: the look (`.sidebar`, provisional; the standalone group's chevron FINAL, record 311)

Alloy's binding answer: `.sidebar` (design section 9a.2); MetalUI offers one look and no `.listStyle`. Measured (`P-L1`..`P-L4`, light and dark, data and content forms identical):

- **Rows:** 32 pt high (default and `.inset` are 24), the first row 10 pt below the top, no gap between rows, the rows' spacing is 0; the SwiftUI `Text` label is 16 tall.
- **Indentation:** 12 pt per level; chevron cell 13 pt wide at x 12 (level 0), 24 (level 1), 36 (level 2), the row's full height; label leading x 25, 37, 49 for depths 0, 1, 2. A leaf's label is at the SAME x as its sibling group's (`P-L2`: Z at 25 like A and M, A/C at 37 like A/B, A/B/D at 49): **a leaf reserves the chevron slot**; a leaf has no cell. A list with no hierarchy has its labels at 16 and no chevron cell.
- **Chevron:** the glyph is swapped, not rotated (see OG-H); collapsed ink 5 x 8 pt at (17, 23) of the first row's frame, expanded 8.5 x 5 at (14.5, 26); colour a mid grey, sampled #808080 at alpha 0.94 (light) and #7C7C7C (dark) (the sampled ink over a transparent snapshot).
- **Selection:** the highlight spans x 10 to 290 of 300 (a 10 pt inset on each side), the full row height (top and bottom inset 0, rows abut), corner radius about 8 pt (a 2x corner profile fit, r = 16 px); the row view is `ListTableRowView` and the rounding is drawn. **The highlight COLOUR was not measured**: the snapshot of the selected row is opaque black (it blends against the sidebar material, which `cacheDisplay` does not capture) and the probe's process is not the active app; the AppKit system colours (selectedContentBackground #0059D1, unemphasizedSelectedContentBackground #464646, accent #007AFF) are printed beside it and are NOT the drawing. **Gate:** lane 4 does not ship a selection colour until a probe reads the drawn pixels (a `CGWindowList` capture or an active-app run, see human check group OG).
- **Standalone `DisclosureGroup` (eager contexts, lane 1):** 24 pt label row; chevron at the leading edge (collapsed ink 5 x 8 at 2 pt in, expanded 8 x 5 at 0), label ink at 12.5 pt from the group's leading edge (14 with a `Label` icon); light chevron #C0C0C0 / #BFBFBF at alpha 0.93, the label #000000 at alpha 0.83; the content starts at +24 under the label row, 0 spacing between children, **no indentation: the content is a centre-aligned stack in the group's width** (a nested group starts at its parent's x). This standalone look is not the list's look and the two do not share an indent rule.
- **Lane 1 as landed:** a 24 pt row (`Button` `.plain`, an `HStack` with a `Spacer`, `.frame(height: 24)`), the chevron `Image(systemName: "chevron.right" / "chevron.down")` (a glyph swap, `P-L3`; the bundled symbol route draws it off macOS) at 9 pt semibold, centred in a 9 pt cell inside a 12 pt slot, so the label starts 12 from the leading edge (the probe's ink at 12.5); content a centre-aligned `VStack` (spacing 0) under the row, no indentation. **Colour: `.tertiary`** (the secondary-label level 0.306 light, 0.292 dark, about #B1B1B1 over white), the nearest existing semantic level to the probe's standalone chevron (#C0C0C0, #BFBFBF light; the dark #FFFFFF was an unchecked sample); **the list's own chevron (#808080 light, #7C7C7C dark, `P-L3`) is the lane 4 look and is a different grey**. Geometry is asserted in layout boxes (the glyph's box is 8 x 10 right and 10 x 7 down, whose ink is the probe's 5 x 8 and 8 x 5 only approximately); the ink size and colour against the real control stay a human check (`OG`).
