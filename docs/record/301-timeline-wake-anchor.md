# 301 - the timeline clock anchors on a frame (TW-A)

Class M, no divergence. Alloy glass-on typing showed a `TimelineSchedule` state dirtying on every key frame and a tick per frame while idle (`FP-S` named it): a wake firing before its frame's clock reached the deadline, so each frame noted the same due deadline again. `TimelineStore.noteDeadline` anchored `clockOffset` on the deadline (up to 0.5 s ahead of the frame), not on the frame. Now `noteDeadline(atTimestamp:frameTimestamp:owner:stateTable:)` anchors on the frame and converts a deadline as frame date plus distance (the real clock plus distance at timestamp 0). The headless simulated clock (`DX-A`) is anchored before any frame, so the bug showed only on the wall clock: the pins use `testingAnchorTimelineOnWallClock()` with a recording scheduler, red under the old anchor (wake delay about 0 against 0.5 s). Headless frames per key were one with and without a backdrop on the simulated clock, before and after; the wall-clock repro (real `Task` scheduler, ticks at the wall rate) showed a due wake on every tick before the fix.

Ruling `TW-A` in `docs/superpowers/2026-11-13-timeline-wake-decisions.md`. To confirm in the app: a glass run with `METALUI_LATENCY_TRACE_ALL=1` should show no `state@TimelineSchedule/1` ticks outside the blink and no `why=needsRedraw` on spaced keys.
