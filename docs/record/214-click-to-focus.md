## 2. What changed

`Handlers.focusesOnPress` is `isFocusable` and the interactions contain `.edit` or `.automatic`; `nil` interactions stay out. The `focusable(_:)` spellings record `.automatic` where they recorded `nil`: `Box.swift` (the `StyledElement` one) and `KeyboardModifier.swift` (the layer one; the proposal-path wrapper only delegates to it, so there are two independent write sites, not three). Doc comments updated at `Box.swift`, `KeyPress.swift`, `KeyboardModifier.swift`, `ClipboardModifiers.swift`, `ColorPicker.swift`, `Handlers.swift`. Controls (`Button`, `Toggle`, `Slider`, `Stepper`, `Picker`, `ColorPicker`) set `isFocusable` directly and record no interactions, so they stay out **by two layers**: absent interactions, and no keyboard attachment at all (so `Frame.registerHandlers` registers no press region for them). No `Window`, `Frame`, `HoverKeyRegion` or retained-path source change: `focusOnPress` already had the ladder and the gates, and the retained ledger already tallies `focusOnPress`.

Docs: divergence 94 retired (row, retired table, header paragraph, record 04 section), 57 amended with a pin; `CLAUDE.md` + `AGENTS.md` 207 live; `docs/guides/input.md`, `docs/api-overview.md`, `docs/migration.md` (a row), `docs/rules/input-focus.md`, `closeout-inventory-map.tsv` (the `94` references); `Tasks.md` sub-item; human group `FC`.

## 5. Behaviour changes for callers

A `.focusable()` box takes focus on a primary press (migration row), registers one non-opaque press region (hitbox counts and `focusOnPressCount` rise by one each), clears a focused `TextField` (`RS4`), and inside a `.hoverKeyRegion()` focuses instead of clearing. An `onKey` on it hears keys after a click. `.focusable(interactions: .activate)` is the opt-out. A press on the empty space of a focusable stack focuses it where SwiftUI's does not (`C16b`; carried with 57). MetalFlow's full-panel `.focusable()` (`OpenFlowPanel.swift:41`, `SavePanel.swift:51`) now takes click focus.
