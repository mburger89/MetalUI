# 434: a registration cut by a clip its occurrence pushed moves at the viewport edge, and an occurrence whose entry clip changed moves with it (`FS-X`, `FS-Y`; class M, no divergence)

Branch `perf/clipped-registration-fold`, base master `164978ef`. No public API, no divergence (242 live, next label 250).

**Why.** Record 431 found the redos of Alloy's streamed transcript to be `graftedPrepaint` refusals, and the `prepaint` half of them `translationRefused: hitboxClipped` / `accessibilityClipped`: a registration cut by a clip its occurrence opened (the row's rounded chrome) moved only while it stayed strictly inside the ambient clip before and after, so a row crossing the scroll viewport's edge refused, the frame was redone and the row quarantined for 8 builds and more.

**What.** `FS-X` (`docs/superpowers/2026-10-20-finer-scopes-decisions.md`): `PrepaintLog` journals clip pushes and pops while a recorder is open (`PrepaintClip` = `InnerClip` + the emission range; the frame's hitbox and emission counts are the ranges), a ledger keeps them as `innerClips` only when a hitbox or emission sits at a depth other than its entry's, and `PrepaintClipFold` (`RetainedTranslationCarries.swift`) re-derives the cut with `Frame.pushedClip` at the old key (which must reproduce the recorded rect bit for bit, else `…NotFolded`) and the new, where the strictly-inside fast path refuses. Refusals: `accessibilityNotFolded`, `hitboxNotFolded`, `accessibilityUnmapped`, `hitboxUnmapped`, `rootClip`, `inexactTranslation`. Threaded through `movedAccessibility`, `shiftedLocals` and `movedHitbox`; `replayPrepaintLedger` re-notes a replayed ledger's clips (`layoutOrigin` for verify mode).

**Path B (`FS-Y`).** The transcript's last redos were not all masks. The streaming row's second paragraph is a grafted `.equatable()` occurrence under the row's own clip, which **grows** as the row streams: its ledger key differed from the frame's in `rects`, `interior`, `ambient.clip` and `ambient.offset`, `shift(to:)` answered `nil` (it needs every ambient field but the offset equal), the prepaint missed, the grafted occurrence could not run it and the frame was redone. `FS-Y` extends the key relation to "moved (possibly not at all), and the entry clip changed" and recomputes every cut from the new entry clip with the arithmetic a fresh frame uses: `clippedShift` on both keys, the old rect of a registration tested against the old clip and its moved rect against the new, `PrepaintClipFold` folding each position from its own key's clip, and the paint record's entry-depth leaves taking the new clip's mask once they are exactly the old one's. No stored bytes; the same gates refuse by name.

**Cause 2, tried and reverted.** A first attempt (`f1a90ad8`, `916b72cd`, `a54e3910`, reverted) made a crossing `CM-B` mask scope transparent to the recorder below it so a row crossing a corner zone could move. It did not meet its target (requests 593 to 569, redos still 4) and needs per-range anchors and a verify-mode prediction of unit ranges; the design and what finishing it needs are carried in the `FS-` document's Carried section, not ruled. The two redos that remain (rows 45 and 46: `maskCrossingChanged`, `innerScope`) are that work.
