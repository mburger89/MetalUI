**What.** `.contextMenu(forSelectionType:menu:primaryAction:)` with SwiftUI's signature (`contextMenu<I: Hashable, M>(forSelectionType: I.Type = I.self, menu: (Set<I>) -> M, primaryAction: ((Set<I>) -> Void)? = nil)`, checked against the SDK swiftinterface; `menu` is a `@MenuContentBuilder`, both closures `@MainActor`) on a `StyledElement` (a `List` carrying it) and over proposal content (an `EnvironmentScope`, every `List` below taking it). On both list forms, the data `List(_:selection:rowHeight:)` and the content/outline `List`: a secondary press on a row builds the menu with `row in selection ? selection : [row]` and does not select (`P-M1`, `P-M1b`); on the list's empty space the set is `[]` and the menu still opens (`P-M2`); a double-click selects with the first click and runs `primaryAction([row])` on the second, a group row not toggling (`P-M3`, `P-M3b`, `P-C1`); Return runs `primaryAction(selection)` (`P-M4`, `P-M4b`); a single click never runs it; with no `primaryAction` nothing more happens; a modifier of another identifier type is inert. `ClickDispatch.clickCount` is published with the modifiers.

**How.** `ContextMenuSelection.swift`: `SelectionContextMenu`, the modifier's payload type-erased over `I` (`Set<AnyHashable>` closures, `itemType` for the match), its three products (`listAttachment` for the empty space, `rowAttachment`, `clickHandler`) and `returnKey`; `ListSelection.currentSet()`; `ControlKeys.isPrimaryActionKey`; an environment key `selectionContextMenu`. `ContextualAttachment` gained `selection` (carried through `.help`, replaced by a later `.contextMenu(menuItems:)`). Each list reads `handlers.contextual?.selection ?? environmentTop.selectionContextMenu` in layout, keeps it only when `applies(to: ID.self)`, and sets the row boxes' (`List.swift`) or row records' (`ListContent.swift`'s `unitWrapper`) `handlers.contextual` and `onClick`, its own region's attachment, and a Return branch in its `onKey` (after the caller's `onKey`, before the arrows). Click count: `Window.runClick(_:on:modifiers:clickCount:)` from `dispatchClick`'s and `runRowSelectionClick`'s mouse-up and from `GestureCallback.click(..., clickCount:)` (the arena's click leaf stores the release's count in `pressClickCount`); `ClickDispatch.running(modifiers:clickCount:)` restores both after the handler. **SDL needed no plumbing**: `SDLPlatform` already carries `SDL_MouseButtonEvent.clicks` into `MouseEvent.clickCount` on both down and up (`IF-O`).

**Choices where the probe was silent** (`OG-N`). The action is exactly the second click (count 2; a third click acts nothing). MetalUI acts in the second click's release, not ~60 ms later (SwiftUI's is AppKit's deferral, unobservable). Return with an empty selection runs nothing and is not consumed. A flat data list is sized to its rows, so it has no empty space of its own to press (the content forms have the first row's 10 point inset, and a list given more room gets the `[]` menu); a list with no selection binding still gets the row menu and the double-click action, a selection of `[]`. The modifier on a non-list `StyledElement` is inert (wrap proposal content to reach a list below it).
