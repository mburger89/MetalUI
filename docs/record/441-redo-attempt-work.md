# 441: `FrameWork.redone*`, the work of the attempts a redo abandoned (class M, no divergence, no ruling)

Branch `feat/redo-attempt-work`, base master `ae044e77`. Six public fields, no divergence (242 live, next label 250). Number 440 is `perf/focus-keyed-replay`'s.

**Why (Alloy).** When a frame is redone (`RK-D`, the rollback of record 417, the scoped redo of record 423) the window abandons its first attempt and builds again. `FrameWork` reads its counts from the adopted `Frame`, and the counters are per `Frame`, so the abandoned attempt's prepaints, paints and requests vanished from the report. A redo that replays the rest of the frame prepaints little: Alloy saw 71 to 87 `elementPrepaints` on redone frames against about 124 on their neighbours, so a benchmark that compared a tree with redos against one without was biased in favour of the redos, and removing the redos looked like a 4 percent prepaint regression.

**Investigation.** Which fields already include the abandoned attempt: **none of the `Frame` counters.** `buildFrame()` makes a fresh `Frame` for every attempt and `redoAbandonedFrame` replaces the first with the second. `RedoCounterTests` R3 says it: a redone frame's `elementRequests`, `elementPrepaints` and `layoutMeasureCalls` equal the next (backoff) build's, the redo's alone. The brief's guess that `elementRequests` already counted both was wrong; Alloy's requests are higher on redone frames because the adopted attempt has every replay off (window-wide) or rebuilds the failing rows (scoped), not because the first attempt is in them. The two things that do carry across attempts are deliberate and unchanged: `contentEvaluations` (the redo skips the getters the attempt evaluated, `CF-C`, `carriedEvaluations`) and the retained counters of the window (`retainedRedos`, `retainedRedoEscalations`). The rest of `FrameWork` (lazy, outline, code, table and mask counters, `retained*` hits) is the adopted attempt's, by the same construction; the rasterizer's pixels are the window's and count both. Where the dropped `Frame` dies: `redoAbandonedFrame` (`Window.swift`), at `frame = buildFrame()` (and again at the escalation's third build); verify mode (`RK-V`) already reports the first build's element counters through `adoptElementWork` and is untouched.

**What.** `Frame.abandonedWork` (`AbandonedWork`: six `Int`s, `Frame.swift`) is filled by `redoAbandonedFrame` from each frame it drops, before it is dropped: the first attempt, and the second when it asked for a redo again (so the sum is over every abandoned attempt). It is read by `testingOnFrameAdopted` into `BuildWork`, summed over the builds by `TestWindow`'s `pending`, and held by `FrameWork` as `redoneElementRequests`, `redoneElementPrepaints`, `redoneElementPaints`, `redoneLayoutMeasureCalls`, `redoneLayoutCacheHits` and `redoneLayoutCacheMisses` (doc comments, `init` arguments defaulting to 0, terms in `+`), the way `maskScopesPushed` was wired in record 436. **No existing field changed meaning.** A frame that is not redone never runs the capture: the struct is six zero `Int`s inside `Frame`, and the hook reads them as integers. In `FrameWork` the six counts sit **out of line**, behind one optional reference (`RedoneBox`, compared by value, nil for a frame not redone; the public fields are computed properties, a write of the value already held changes nothing, so adding 0 never allocates). First version, six stored `Int`s: stride 552 to 600, which moved the growth schedule of the harness's history array (a `[FrameWork]` over-allocates by malloc slack: capacities 1, 2, 4, 4, 9, 9, 9, 9, 9, 18 at 552 bytes, 1, 2, 4, 4, 8, 8, 8, 8, 17 at 600) and so added one allocation (1562 to 1563) to `snappingAddsNoAllocationsAndNoElementRequestsToIdleAndScrolledFrames`, a pin on the harness's own array, not on the frame; the box (stride 560, same schedule through 19 frames) leaves it at 1562. `theRedoneWorkFieldsAddNothingToAnIdleFrame` pins the stride.

Not carried (a follow-up if a client asks): the lazy, outline, code, table and mask counters of the dropped attempt, and the layout memo and signature counters.

**Observed (`FrameWorkRedoneWorkTests`).** A window-wide redo of two cards under an opacity that flips (the `RedoCounterTests` fixture without its counter): the dropped attempt replayed both cards' prepaint and did 3 prepaints, 3 paints and 2 requests, exactly the steady frame before the change; the adopted attempt did 59 prepaints, 42 paints and 58 requests, and 59 prepaints is what the retention-off window prepaints for the same state. `elementPrepaints + redoneElementPrepaints` is 62 = 59 + 3, the two attempts counted from outside the redo. In Alloy's chrome transcript (`ctOuterScopePair`, 40 streamed words) the two scoped redos report the dropped attempt's prepaints, paints, requests and 82 layout counts; every frame without a redo reports 0 in all six.

**Cost.** The idle frame of a clipped two-leaf island on master `ae044e77` and here: 5 element requests and 222 allocations both ways; 19,322 bytes to 19,378 (the 8-byte pointer in each stored `FrameWork`, as the history array's buffers round). `FrameWorkMaskCountersTests`' island, 25,746 bytes on this branch against its 25,922 bound. No pin or ceiling moved.
