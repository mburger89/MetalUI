## 1. What was wrong and what was built

An `.equatable()` occurrence whose layout origin or scroll offset moved (K1/K2, `FS-D`) **and** whose enclosing render effect changed its affine (`RZ-`) in the same frame fell through both translation arms: the prepaint counted `effectAndLayoutMoved` and ran, the paint missed. MetalCreator's case is a header growing above a zooming canvas.

**Built (`EL-B`, `EL-C`).** One relation, `shiftAndEffects` (`ReplayKey` for paint, `PrepaintKey` for prepaint), delegating to the old `shift` when the effects are equal so the layout arm cannot move, and to the effect arm when the rects are equal. Replay is `effect_now(local + delta)`: the whole-device-pixel delta (`FS-M` exactness) is added in the occurrence's own pre-effect space (leaf rects, hitbox `bounds`/`origin`/`unclipped`, accessibility locals, a geometry note's `moved`, element bounds) and the effect now in force is applied second by the live scopes (paint, through `processScopes`) or by re-derivation (`Frame.rederived`, the per-hitbox helper extracted from `translatedHitboxes` with outputs unchanged; accessibility through `translatedAccessibility`; geometry through `remade`). Lane A is the paint half; lane B the prepaint half: `Frame.movedCombinedLedger`, `shiftedLocals`, `movedCombinedGeometryNotes` (`RetainedTranslationCarries.swift`), the combined branch of `retainedPrepaintDecision`. The ledger and the record rebase to the **whole new key** with the moved locals, so the next unchanged frame is an exact hit (`EL-G`); the pointer is read against the moved hitboxes (`FS-P`, `RZ-D`).

**Refusals the combined arm inherits or adds, all by name:** `effectFlatteningChanged`, `effectClipChanged`, `effectScopeChanged` and the other effect refusals of `RZ-` unchanged; `presentationAnchor` and `contextMenus` even for a pure-translation effect (`EL-F`); `pressOnly`; hitbox `hitboxShape`/`hitboxScrolls`/`hitboxHandlers`/`hitboxClipped`; `geometryNamedSpace` (`EL-E`); `inexactTranslation`; and the new **`effectAndLayoutInner`** for a hitbox, accessibility record or geometry note made inside an effect the occurrence opened (`EL-D`: such a map is anchored at the occurrence's own position). **`effectAndLayoutMoved` is retired** (never emitted, pinned). `hitboxUnderEffect`/`accessibilityUnderEffect` stay for the layout-only arm (`EL-I`). The graft is untouched (`EL-H`) and grafts across a combined frame.

**Latent defect found (`EL-L`):** `movedPaintRecord` kept a moved final stream under a prepaint key whose ambient never holds the paint scopes; fixed in lane A. `EL-M`, `EL-N` record how the paint and prepaint count one refusal once.
