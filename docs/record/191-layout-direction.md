## What landed

- **Kernel (`MetalUILayout`).** `LayoutTree.registersRightToLeft` (a flag `Frame` keeps equal to the environment in effect) marks each node at `appendNode`
  in `rightToLeftNodes`; `placeNative` reflects every child's rect about the middle of the marked parent's bounds (`mirroredChildRect`, through the `place`
  helper; `placeCustom` and `placeGrid` the same). `nodeSignature` mixes a tag for marked nodes only (left-to-right signatures bit-identical), segments and
  grafts carry the marks, `structureDigest` prints `|rtl`, `reset` clears. Measurement is untouched: equal counted work (`mirroringDoesTheSameMeasurementWorkAsLeftToRight`).
- **Frame (`MetalUI`).** `environmentTop`'s `didSet` sets/clears the flag (root setter, `withEnvironment` push and pop). `RenderEffectSpec.affine(in:mirrored:)` conjugates
  offset/scale/rotation by the reflection, read at the three sites (prepaint, paint, `effectBoundingBox`). `resolveTextStyle` swaps `.leading`/`.trailing`.
- **Horizontal scrolling.** The offset is logical (from the leading edge): `ScrollChrome.translation(offset:)`, `indicatorBounds`, `Hitbox.scrollMirrored` (wheel sign in `Window.scroll`),
  `ScrollerFrame.mirrored`/`logicalStart(of:)` used by `noteScrollTarget`, `scrollOffset(bringing:into:anchor:)` and the lazy planner; the flag is appended to the prepaint extras vector (index 9).
- **Demo.** A right-to-left section in `METALUI_LOOKS_DEMO=1` (`LayoutDirectionDemo.swift`, its own `@inline(never)` functions, `CI-AI`).
- **Docs.** Divergence 25 retired and 1045 added (`divergences.md`, record 04, record 05 rows, inventory map rows 45/158 now class A), decisions doc, prefix row, rule paragraph in
  `docs/rules/layout.md`, `docs/migration.md`, `docs/api-overview.md`, human-check group LD, `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` (reserved range and group list).

## Plan deviations

- The plan's `Tests/MetalUITests/LayoutDirectionTests.swift` window tests were written over the public harness (`Tests/MetalUITestingTests/LayoutDirectionWindowTests.swift`, plain imports, runs on
  every platform); only the Frame-level halves (root environment, effect conjugation, resolved text style) are `@testable` in `Tests/MetalUITests/LayoutDirectionTests.swift`. The graft pin is in the
  kernel file, not `GraftKernelTests.swift`.
- **Not tested: `Canvas` and `Image` not mirrored.** MetalUI has no `Canvas` type, and an `Image` is never mirrored (SwiftUI's default), so there is no mechanism to pin; the probe measures both (`O2`, `N1`, `N2`) and divergence 1045 states them.
- The probe's K arm differs from the plan's draft (three siblings around an inner stack, not four): 90 / inner 60-70 / 50 against the plan's 90 / 60..90 / 60 / 70; the semantics are the same.
- `flipsForRightToLeftLayoutDirection(_:)` is not offered (plan §3). The demo was compiled and its tree is built by `everyProductionTreeBuildsOnAOneMegabyteThread`; it was not looked at by a person (group LD).
