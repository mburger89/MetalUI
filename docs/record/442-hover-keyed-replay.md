# 442: a retained occurrence replays while what its paint reads of the hover is unchanged (`RK-AR`; class M, no divergence)

Branch `perf/hover-keyed-replay`, base master `40751434` (#457, focus-keyed replay, record 440, is in it). No public API, no divergence (243 live, next label 251). Ruling `RK-AR`; next `RK-AS`.

**Why (Alloy).** Streaming a long reply with the pointer resting over a code fence's horizontal scroller left one redo: `pointerInMovedLedger` on the `ReplyPiece` occurrence `m16#0-md130`, about 4 element requests a step on a baseline of 114.6. The occurrence's ledger holds the fence scroller's hitbox, the scroll moves it under the pointer, the moved-ledger arm refuses the prepaint replay, and a grafted occurrence that cannot replay its prepaint is the redo. Nothing under it reads hover: no fence, scroller or copy button has a hover value, a handler that reads it, a pointer style or a help.

**Findings.** (1) Nothing in a prepaint ledger reads the pointer: `hoveredHitbox` is `nil` until `resolveHover` runs between prepaint and paint, `PrepaintPass` has no `isHovered`, and `mousePosition` is read only by the retained decisions, so the hover clause of `retainedSubtreeHasPointerOrPress` was dead at prepaint and at the graft; the graft's live guard was the pointer against the hitboxes where they stood. (2) Everything the window does with the pointer (`onHover`, `onContinuousHover`, the cursor, tooltips, wheel routing and its latch, click) is a function of `lastHitboxes` plus the pointer, and a replayed or moved ledger must produce the same hitbox list (verify mode compares fingerprints). One gap, not caused by this change: `Hitbox.fingerprint` records only which handler members exist, so a stale pointer-style value or help text was invisible to verify mode. (3) The only hover-dependent output inside an occurrence is its paint, through `PaintPass.isHovered`: `resolvedForPointerState` (the hover slot of background and border), the glass press and `Button` (both only while active, itself a refusal), `NativeTappable`'s hover colour. Every reader asks with its own id. (4) Rejected: refusing only where hover-sensitive handlers, a cursor or a tooltip are present (those are window-side and need no protection, and the real dependency, a hover background, is a decoration); keying on "which element under me is hovered" as `RK-AQ` does for focus (Alloy's record was made with nothing hovered and the scroller is hovered at the new position, so it still misses, and the prepaint cannot compute the hovered element exactly: a later registration may cover it).

**What changed.**
- `RetainedHover.swift` (new): `HoverReads` (`readers` at most 32, `hovered`, `wholeSubtree`), `answer(hovered:under:)`, `reads(_:)`; `RetainLog.noteHover` (log limit 256); `Frame.noteHoverRead`, `noteHoverReadByHitbox`, `noteReplayedHoverReads` (`@inline(never)`), `closedHoverReads`, `retainedHoverReaderUnderPointer`, `retainedHoverNeedsPrepaint`, `retainedSubtreeHasPressOrSelection` (the old `retainedSubtreeHasPointerOrPress` without hover; `retainedSubtreeHasPointerPressOrFocus` is gone, the graft spells its conjuncts).
- `PaintPass.isHovered` notes its read; `resolvedForPointerState` asks only where a hover value is declared; `EquatableComponent.paintGroup`'s `.hit` arm hands the replayed record's readers to the enclosing recorders; `finishRetainedPaint` stores `ContentMemoStore.Entry.recordHover` (not a record field: 248 bytes is pinned), taints `hoverReadOutside`, and traps in verify mode when a fresh paint's reads differ from the record's.
- The paint decision: `underPointer` is `underPress` (trace `press`) and `hoverMoved` (trace `hover`). The prepaint decision: `pointerInLedger` is a record painted with a reader hovered or the pointer over a reader's hitbox; the three moved arms (`pointerInMovedLedger`) test the moved hitboxes for readers only and only when the occurrence is not grafted. `graftRecord` and `flushPendingGrafts` refuse a record painted with a reader hovered.
- Verify mode: `RetainedVerifySnapshot.pointer` (style and visibility regions through `Window.forEachPointerStyleRegion`, now also what `pointerUnderPointer` runs, and the help text and provider presence through `Window.contextualTarget(in:at:where:)`); testing hooks `testingCorruptPrepaintLedgers` channels `pointerStyle` and `help` (`Hitbox.handlers` is a `var` for them), `testingCorruptHoverReads`, `testingRecordHovers`.
